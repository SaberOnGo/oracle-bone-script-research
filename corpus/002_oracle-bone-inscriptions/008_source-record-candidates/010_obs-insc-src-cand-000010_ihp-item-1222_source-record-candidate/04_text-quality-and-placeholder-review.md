## Placeholder distinction / 占位符区别

The three inline PNG files are rendered glyph responses embedded in the
source prose. A rendered glyph response may preserve a displayed sign, but
it does not show surrounding bone texture, neighboring signs, scale, or the
sign's physical location.

三份内嵌 PNG 是嵌入来源文字的页面渲染字形响应。它们可能保留页面所示
字形，但不显示骨面纹理、相邻字、比例或实体位置。
