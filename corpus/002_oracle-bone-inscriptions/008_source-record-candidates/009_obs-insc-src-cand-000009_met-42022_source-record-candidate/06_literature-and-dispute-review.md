## Concrete literature checks / 具体文献待查

- Which catalog first records accession `18.56.71`?
- Is the object reproduced in Heji or another plate corpus?
- Does a collection history identify a previous owner or acquisition route?
- Which scholar, if any, proposed a reading for the visible marks?
- Are there contrary readings, authenticity notes, or conservation reports?
- Which publication supplies findspot, batch, period, or group context?

- 哪部著录首次记录馆藏号 `18.56.71`？
- 本对象是否见于《合集》或其他图版语料？
- 收藏史是否记录前藏者或入藏路线？
- 哪位学者曾提出可见刻痕的释读？
- 是否有相反释读、真伪说明或保护记录？
- 哪篇文献提供出土地、批次、时期或组类语境？

are checked, literature status is
`bibliography_and_dispute_review_pending`.

在上述路线核对前，不把任何文献主张转成项目释读。文献状态为
