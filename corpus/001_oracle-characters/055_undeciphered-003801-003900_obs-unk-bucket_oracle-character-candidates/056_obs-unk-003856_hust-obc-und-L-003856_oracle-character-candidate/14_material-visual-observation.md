English:
The upper portion forms a pointed or arched outline with a light interior.
Several narrow strokes occupy the center and extend below the outline. The
lower area separates into short angled traces on both sides. The dark edges
are uneven, with light gaps between the central and side strokes.

中文：
上部形成尖状或拱形轮廓，内部保持明亮。中心有几条较窄痕迹，并向轮廓下方
延伸。下部向两侧分成短斜向痕迹。深色边缘不均匀，中心与两侧痕迹之间有亮
色间隙。
