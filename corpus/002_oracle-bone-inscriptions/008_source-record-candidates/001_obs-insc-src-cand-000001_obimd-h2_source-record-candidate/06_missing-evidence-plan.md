# Missing Evidence And Next Checks / 缺失证据与后续核查

## English

This dossier is reproducible at the OBIMD package level but not complete at
the archaeological or catalog level. The next checks are concrete:

The Heji-number question now has a high-confidence candidate answer: Heji 2.
The next task is to open the provenance-bearing Heji plate and page.

1. Open the exact Heji 2 edition volume, plate, and page, and compare its
   printed object against the National Library and OBIMD images.
2. Identify the edition, volume, plate, and page from which the rubbing and
   reference transcription were drawn. Preserve page-level evidence.
3. Establish the collection institution and object number from an independent
   catalog or museum record; do not infer them from the file name.
4. Find the findspot or excavation context, including pit or batch when a
   cited archaeological record supplies it.
5. Verify the period and group classification against a named published
   system and record disagreements rather than selecting one silently.
6. Find a readable transcription or OCR source and record its editor, page,
   text quality, missing signs, and disputed positions.
7. Resolve the OBIMD rights conflict before publishing any image derivative.

## 简体中文

本档案在 OBIMD 来源包层面可以复跑，但在考古和著录层面尚不完整。后续应
完成以下具体核查：

《合集》号问题现已有高置信候选答案：《合集》2。首项任务转为打开带
来源链的《合集》图版和页码。

1. 打开《合集》2 的确切版本、卷、图版和页码，并将印刷对象与国图和
   OBIMD 图像复核。
2. 查明拓片和参考释文所据版本、卷次、图版号和页码，保存页级证据。
3. 从独立著录或博物馆记录核对馆藏机构和对象号，不从文件名推断。
4. 核对出土地或发掘语境；如考古记录提供坑位或批次，也须一并保存。
5. 依有名称和引文的分类系统核对时期和组类归属；有分歧时并列记录。
6. 寻找可读释文或 OCR 来源，记录整理者、页码、文本质量、缺字和争议位置。
7. 在公开任何图片派生件之前，先解决 OBIMD 权利说明冲突。

## Stop Conditions / 停止条件

Do not assign a formal inscription ID merely because a number resembles a
catalog number. Do not fill a missing field with a probable value. Record a
failed search route, access date, and source checked when evidence is absent.

不得因为编号看似著录号就分配正式卜辞 ID，不得用可能值填补缺失字段。
找不到证据时，应记录查过的来源、访问日期和失败路线。
