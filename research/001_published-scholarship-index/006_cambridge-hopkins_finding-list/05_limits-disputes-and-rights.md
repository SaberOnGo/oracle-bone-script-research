## Rights And Reuse / 权利与复用

Effective status is `metadata_only_until_verified`.

The public page and its statement that bones were digitised are not a rights
clearance. This dossier does not copy collection images, 3D assets, full OCR,
the full finding-list text, or long passages. Before any such material is
saved or redistributed, record the exact asset terms, provider, object ID,
size, checksum, and allowed derivative.

网页可访问，以及页面称甲骨已数字化，都不是权利许可。本档案不复制
馆藏图像、3D 资产、全文 OCR、完整目录文本或长段原文。保存或再分发
任何此类材料前，必须逐项记录资产条款、提供方、对象 ID、大小、校验和
及允许的派生形式。

This dossier is not a rights clearance, not a transcription, not a reading,
not an object-identity decision, and not a decipherment conclusion.

本档案不是权利许可，不是释文，不是释读，不是实物同一性裁决，也不是
