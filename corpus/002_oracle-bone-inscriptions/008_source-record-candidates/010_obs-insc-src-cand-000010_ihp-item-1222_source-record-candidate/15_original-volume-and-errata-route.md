## Why this is correction-controlled / 为何受勘误控制

The 2013 companion sample says that the 2011 plate volume contains 1,028
regular join plates and 14 supplements. It also says that later work found
misplaced plates, erroneous tracings, wrong numbers, omissions, mistaken
joins, and added joins. A later CiNii and holding review identifies
`Tuban pian kanwu` as a distinct physical part. The plate, separate errata,
and companion are therefore separate source gates.

2013 年配套卷样页称，2011 年图版卷收 1,028 组常规缀合图版和 14 组
补遗。样页同时说明后续发现图版错位、误摹、错号、漏收、误收和新加缀。
后续 CiNii 与馆藏复核确认《图版篇勘误》是独立实物分卷，因此图版、
独立勘误和配套考释必须分别通过来源门槛。

The public four-page sample does not contain plate 1028. Searches inside the
sample find no `Yi 6087` or `Heji 13517`. Its only Arabic `1028` occurrence
states the total number of regular groups. It is not a plate locator and does
not show that group 1028 escaped correction.

公开的四页样本不含第 1028 组图版。样本内也检索不到《乙》6087 或
《合》13517。阿拉伯数字 `1028` 唯一一次出现是常规组总数；它不是图版
定位，也不能证明第 1028 组未受勘误影响。

## Lawful acquisition route / 合法取得路线

Use the smallest reproducible request, in this order:

1. Ask the holding library for the page containing `Zhuihui 1028`, its facing
   caption, and the relevant 2013 correction and commentary entry.
2. Request the 2018 article at pages 20-29 through a library document service.
3. If page service is unavailable, consult or purchase the two ISBN-identified
   volumes; record invoice or request ID without committing personal data.
4. Capture the title page, copyright page, target plate, correction entry,
   and adjacent page numbers. Keep raw captures in the ignored archive.
5. Record checksum, byte size, access route, rights response, and page mapping
   before any claim review.

按以下顺序提出最小、可复跑的请求：

1. 向馆藏机构申请《缀彙》1028 所在页、相邻图注，以及 2013 年对应勘误
   与考释条目。
2. 通过图书馆文献服务申请 2018 年论文第 20-29 页。
3. 若不能按页服务，再查阅或购买两个 ISBN 对应卷；记录订单或申请号，
   但不把个人资料提交到仓库。
4. 捕获书名页、版权页、目标图版、勘误条目和相邻页码；原始捕获留在
   忽略区。
5. 在任何主张复核前，记录校验和、大小、取得路线、权利回复和页码对应。

## Promotion and rejection gate / 晋级与否决门槛

Promotion requires four opened sources: the 2011 target plate, the separate
`Tuban pian kanwu` entry, the matching 2013 commentary, and the 2018 article
at pages 20-29. The plate must also be mapped to the opened IHP object images
without geometric warping.

晋级必须打开四项来源：2011 年目标图版、独立《图版篇勘误》条目、
2013 年对应考释和 2018 年论文第 20-29 页；还必须在不作几何形变的
条件下，把图版映射到已打开的史语所实物图。

Reject or withhold the route if the group number is absent, renumbered,
corrected to different fragments, based only on a remote join, or lacks a
traceable image-to-object mapping. Until then, join verdict, reading,
genealogy, and authenticity remain `withheld`.

若组号缺失、改号、勘误成其他残片、仅为遥缀，或无法追溯图像到实物的
映射，则否决或继续暂缓该路线。在此之前，缀合裁决、释文、谱系与真伪
一律保持 `withheld`。
