## Source-reported description / 来源说明

The English page says a character described as “turtle” survives on the
fragment. It states that oracle-bone turtle forms can be shown in profile or
overview, and describes plastrons as important divination material in Shang
culture. It also mentions the large YH127 plastron and four related-script
examples: overview turtle, frog, spider, and scorpion used as a loan for ten
thousand.

中文页说反面龟甲残存一“龟”字，甲骨文有侧视和俯视全龟两种字形；并说明龟版是商代
占卜的重要材料，列出殷墟 YH127 大腹甲和龟、黾、鼄、万等相关字形。以上均是博物
馆来源说明，不是本项目的新翻译、字形归并或已确认释读。

The captured pages provide no complete line-by-line transcription. No OCR
correction, palaeographic normalization, or new reading is added here.

页面没有提供逐行完整释文。本页不增加 OCR 修订、文字学规范化或新的释读。
