# Official Artifact Register and Images
# 官方考古登记与实物图像

Review date / 复核日期: `2026-08-28`

Status / 状态: `official composite membership opened`

This dossier opens the IHP Archaeological Data Digital Archive, searches the
registered numbers cited in the literature, and visually checks its official
scale-bearing images. It establishes official artifact and composite
membership records. It does not decide whether every proposed seam is valid.

本档案打开史语所考古资料数位典藏系统，查询文献所引登记号，并目视核查
官方带尺度图像。现可确认正式实物登记与合编成员记录，但不裁决每条拟议
接缝是否成立。

## Official composite membership / 正式合编成员关系

The `ZR038421` page lists seven registered members:

- `R038421`;
- `R039467`;
- `R053740`;
- `R053840`;
- `R054970`;
- `R060751`;
- `R062431`.

Each of the five literature-cited R pages also points back to `ZR038421`.
This establishes `official composite membership`, or `正式合编成员关系`,
at the catalog level.

文献所引五个 R 号详情页也都反向指向 `ZR038421`。因此可在馆藏登记层
确认这些成员关系，而不再只依赖论文转述。

The database also creates `ZR053740` from `R053740` and `R053840`. Both
individual detail pages display the same two-piece front and reverse images.
This independently supports the source's B grouping as an official nested
composite record.

数据库另以 `R053740` 和 `R053840` 建立 `ZR053740`。两个单件详情页都
显示同一组两片正反面图。因此，论文所称 B 组获得正式嵌套合编记录支持。

Catalog membership is not a geometric seam verdict. The two pieces in the B
front image are laid close together but retain a visible gap. Their placement
can be inspected; tooth-by-tooth contact cannot be reproduced from that image.

合编成员关系不等于接缝几何裁决。B 组正面图把两片近置，但仍有可见间隙；
可以检查摆放关系，不能据此复现逐齿接触。

## Source transcription boundary / 来源释文边界

The parent page contains a nine-row inscription table. It distinguishes edge
and rib locations, writing direction, line length, and one practice-carving
row. Several signs are encoded as private numeric entities and cannot be
reliably reproduced as Unicode text.

上层详情页含九行铭文表，区分边甲与肋甲、行向、行字数，并列一条习刻。
多个字以私用数字实体编码，不能可靠转写为 Unicode 文本。

The table is preserved as a checksum-bound source snapshot. Its liding and
transcription are not adopted as project readings or translation.

该表只以绑定校验和的来源快照保存；其中隶定、释文不提升为本项目释读
或译文。

## Visual inspection of official images / 官方图像目视检查

The `ZR038421` front and reverse images show the full assembled layout with
scale and watermark. They are clearer than the museum thumbnail and expose
the large horizontal gaps, the upper narrow section, the left additions, and
the drilling layout on the reverse.

`ZR038421` 正反面原图带尺度和水印，清晰度高于博物馆缩略图，可见横向
大缺口、上方狭长部、左侧新增片，以及反面钻凿布局。

The older `R038421` images show a large earlier composite on a black
background. `R039467` is photographed separately. Their official membership
in `ZR038421` is clear, but the database does not map each of them to the
publication's A-D labels.

较早的 `R038421` 图像显示黑底上的大型旧合编，`R039467` 则单独拍摄。
两者属于 `ZR038421` 已有正式记录，但数据库没有把它们逐一映射到论文
A-D 标签。

The `R053740` image visibly includes labels for both `R053740` and `R053840`.
The front and reverse preserve their relative placement and a `0-3 cm` scale.

`R053740` 图像同时拍到 `R053740`、`R053840` 两个号牌。正反面均保留
相对摆放关系和 `0-3 cm` 比例尺。

`R054970`, `R060751`, and `R062431` each have separate front and reverse
images with a `0-3 cm` scale. `R062431` shows several shallow linear traces
on the outer face and five prepared hollows on the inner face. This is a
surface observation only; no sign identity is assigned.

`R054970`、`R060751`、`R062431` 均有独立正反面图和 `0-3 cm` 比例尺。
`R062431` 外面可见数条浅线，内面可见五处整治凹槽。这里只记录表面现象，
不判定文字身份。

The individual photographs do not share one lighting setup, background, or
orientation. Automated pixel joining would therefore require calibrated
rescaling, rotation, color normalization, and a no-warp control.

各单件照片的光照、背景和朝向并不统一。若以后进行像素拼合，必须先完成
带尺度缩放、旋转、色彩归一和禁止形变的对照。
