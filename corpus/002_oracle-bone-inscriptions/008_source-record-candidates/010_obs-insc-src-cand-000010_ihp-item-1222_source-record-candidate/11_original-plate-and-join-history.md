## Join history reported on page 14 / 第 14 页报告的缀合史

The article reports three earlier stages and its own addition:

1. `Yi Bian 5804`, `5520`, and `5061` were joined as group 295 in
   `Yinxu wenzi zhuihe`, 1955, page 180.
2. `Heji` added `Yi Bian 4817` and cataloged the result as `Heji 13517`.
3. Yan Yiping joined `4817`, `5520`, and `5061` as group 165 but omitted
   `5804`; the cited 1989 book route is page 151.
4. Song reports adding B and C after inspecting the original pieces.

论文报告三个较早阶段及作者新增阶段：

1. 《乙》5804、5520、5061 见《殷虚文字缀合》第 295 组，
   1955 年，第 180 页。
2. 《合集》再加《乙》4817，著录为《合》13517。
3. 严一萍把 4817、5520、5061 缀为第 165 组，但漏收 5804；
   文中所引 1989 年书目路线为第 151 页。
4. 宋雅萍报告在目验实物后加入 B、C 两版。

These are source-reported stages. The earlier books have not yet been opened
in this project, so their group and page statements remain citation routes.

以上均为来源报告。本项目尚未打开两部较早原书，因此组号和页码仍是
待复核引文路线。

## Published seam evidence / 出版接缝依据

Pages 14-15 report that the plate is a left dorsal carapace. The joined area
runs from the first through third rib plates and connects the neck and the
second and fourth edge plates. The article classifies the script as Bin group.

第 14-15 页报告该版为左龟背甲，缀合位置从第一至第三肋甲，并连接颈甲
和第二、第四边甲；论文把字体归入宾组。

For B and C, the author reports a close tooth seam, consistent thickness, and
matching scute pattern. The join also completes a shallow practice `曰` sign.
The author says A and B were first suspected from photographs and then checked
against the original pieces.

对于 B、C，作者报告齿缝密合、厚薄一致、盾纹相合；缀后还补全一处
浅刻习刻 `曰` 字。作者说先从照片怀疑 A、B 可缀，后经实物确认。

These observations are meaningful join criteria. They are not independently
reproduced here because the published plate has no metric seam close-ups.

这些属于有意义的缀合依据，但出版图版没有带尺度的接缝特写，本项目
尚不能独立复现。

## Source transcription only / 仅为来源释文

Page 14 prints eight front lines and one reverse line. Several signs use
special fonts or drawn forms that did not survive text extraction. The safe
diplomatic outline is:

- front line 1 begins `丁卯卜` and ends `于兆`;
- front line 2 ends `于兆。四月`;
- front lines 3 and 4 contain the source string `乎帚奏于兆宅`;
- front lines 5-8 contain damaged or special-form signs;
- the reverse line is printed as `□入□`.

第 14 页印有正面八行、反面一行。多处字使用专用字库或摹写字形，
文字提取未能保留。安全的外交式提要为：

- 正面第 1 行以 `丁卯卜` 开始，以 `于兆` 结束；
- 正面第 2 行以 `于兆。四月` 结束；
- 正面第 3、4 行含来源字符串 `乎帚奏于兆宅`；
- 正面第 5-8 行含残损或专用字形；
- 反面一行印作 `□入□`。

This is `source transcription only` and `仅为来源释文`. It is not project
OCR, a corrected transcription, a translation, or a decipherment result.

## Practice traces reported by the source / 来源报告的习刻痕

Page 15 says A's upper neck area and B show practice traces that were absent
from the rubbings. It reports shallow traces near the upper second rib plate
and edge plate, including forms compared with `口`, `曰`, `丁未`, and `卜`.
The source calls them disordered and not a readable sentence.

第 15 页说 A 上方颈甲与 B 有拓片未显示的习刻痕。第二肋甲上方和边甲
可隐约见与 `口`、`曰`、`丁未`、`卜` 比较的浅痕；来源称其行款凌乱、
文不成句、无法释读。

The source also reports drilling but no burning trace on the reverse, and
therefore proposes partial practice carving. That inference remains the
author's interpretation.

来源还报告反面只见钻凿而无烧灼痕迹，据此提出部分习刻。该推论仍是
作者解释。

## Plate inspection / 图版检查

Page 17 contains a rubbing and a line drawing labeled A, B, and C. The rubbing
shows the same overall silhouette, large horizontal gaps, upper narrow piece,
and right edge as the museum front photograph. This supports a
`plate-layout corroboration candidate`.

第 17 页有标注 A、B、C 的拓片和摹本。其整体外轮廓、横向大缺口、
上方狭长残片和右侧边缘与馆方正面照片相符，形成
`图版布局互证候选`。

The plate is adequate for locating A, B, and C at a coarse level. It is not
adequate for verifying every fragment boundary, faint trace, or the relation
between the three museum-rendered glyph responses and the bone surface.

该图版足以粗略定位 A、B、C，不足以核定每条残片边界、浅刻痕，亦不能
把馆方三个页面渲染字形逐一定位到骨面。

## Independent workshop image / 独立研习会图片

A 2018 workshop report publishes a 282 by 319 pixel composite rubbing labeled
as `Heji 13517` plus `Yi Bian 6087` plus `R60751`. Its outline matches the
2012 plate and museum front view. Because it is low resolution and has no
piece labels, it remains an independent delivery-route corroboration only.

2018 年研习会报道发布一幅 282×319 像素的合成拓片，标为《合》13517
加《乙》6087、`R60751`。其轮廓与 2012 图版和馆方正面图相符；但分辨率
低且无片号标注，只能作为独立传播路线的互证。
