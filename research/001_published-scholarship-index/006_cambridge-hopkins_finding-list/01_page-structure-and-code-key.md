## Key Semantics / 代码含义

The page's `KEY` section defines four cross-reference prefixes:

- `c`: Cambridge University Library (CUL) number.
- `h`: F. H. Chalfant, *The Hopkins Collection of Inscribed Oracle Bone*,
  New York, 1939.
- `j`: Kuo Mo-jo, editor, *Chia ku wen ho chi*, Peking, 1979–1983.
- `y`: Li Hsueh-ch'in and others, editors,
  *Ying-kuo so ts'ang chia ku chi*, Peking, 1985–1991.

页面 `KEY` 区把 `c`、`h`、`j`、`y` 定义为四套馆藏或著录参照。它们是
cross-reference 路由，不是本项目的正式对象 ID。单个号码或同一行并
不能单独证明四套著录指向同一件实物，也不能证明正反面、缀合或释文。

Names, romanization, titles, dates, and editor roles above are
`source-reported`. Exact editions, title forms, pages, plates, and library
holdings remain `unresolved` until the cited works are opened.

以上姓名、罗马字、书名、年代和责任角色均为 `source-reported`。在打开
被引版本前，准确版本、题名形式、页码、图版和馆藏信息仍为
`unresolved`。
