# Limits, disputes, and rights / 限制、争议与权利

## Access limits / 访问限制

The old project routes contain access-restricted or failed automated captures.
The direct Xiaoxuetang pages were readable on 2026-08-21. This difference is
an access-history fact, not proof that every detail endpoint is available.

项目旧路线包含受限页面或自动访问失败记录。2026-08-21 直接小學堂页面
可以读取。这只是访问历史事实，不证明所有详情接口都开放。

No row-level export, complete image inventory, or stable release manifest is
held in this dossier. These are concrete missing materials, not empty fields.

本档案没有保存行级导出、完整图像清单或稳定版本清单。这些是具体缺失
资料，不是用空字段代替。

## Rights boundary / 权利边界

The official license page states that queried glyph images and glyph-attribute
information are dedicated to CC0 1.0 to the extent allowed by law. The page
does not automatically clear every linked dictionary, book scan, catalog image,
or third-party source. Each derivative must retain its exact URL, query route,
date, checksum, and rights note.

官方版权页声明：查询界面所得字形图片及字形属性信息，在法律允许范围内
以 CC0 1.0 放弃权利。该页面不自动清理所有链接字典、书籍扫描、著录图像
或第三方来源。每个派生资料仍需保留确切 URL、查询路线、日期、checksum
和权利说明。

## Scholarly disputes / 学术争议

The database can display alternative forms and link to external dictionaries,
but the reviewed pages do not provide a complete history of proposals,
rejections, or competing readings. A project candidate must not turn a linked
modern label into a confirmed oracle-bone reading.

数据库可以展示不同字形并链接外部字典，但本次复核页面没有提供完整的
提出、否定或异说史。项目候选不得把链接中的今字标签直接改写成已确认
的甲骨文释读。
