# Claim Evidence Gate Review / 命题证据门槛复核

## English

This page applies the project claim-evidence matrix to
`obs-char-000963` on 2026-08-21. It is a human research record for a
candidate object, not a decipherment analysis or a confirmed character
record.

### Evidence items and families

- `E1` is the committed HUST review derivative. Its path, size, dimensions,
  and SHA-256 are recorded in `02_visual-source-index.csv`. It is
  `direct_checked` for visible marks, while its rights remain
  `source_marked_risk_noted`.
- `E2` is the five-member private comparison in
  `17_multi-instance-visual-comparison.md`. The members were opened from
  `large-src-000001` and have member-level checksums. They are one HUST
  archive family, not five independent witnesses.
- `E3` is the complete filename inventory in
  `15_source-filename-evidence-review.md`. It is `direct_checked` for
  member existence and names, but filename parsing is not catalog proof.
- `E4` is the HUST--OBIMD--EvoBC codepoint route for `U+3831`. It is a
  source and dataset lookup family only; it is not independent glyph proof.

### Claim dispositions

- `C1 object identity`: `blocked`. The category and derivative route are
  stable, but a physical object, edition identity, and public-rights chain
  are not established. A dataset category or filename cannot identify a
  bone or fragment.
- `C2 direct glyph observation`: `direct_checked` for E1 and E2. The
  records describe enclosures, stems, branches, breaks, and possible
  damage without naming a component or reading.
- `C3 same-sign, variant, near-form, or component`: `candidate_route` but
  not releasable. Five images share a broad lower outline while their upper
  structures differ. E2 is one archive family; no independent plate-level
  comparison or split rule has cleared the near-form risk.
- `C4 inscription occurrence and context`: `blocked`. Filename candidates
  such as `合20217` and `合30173` have no opened plate, line position, full
  text, neighbouring signs, or verified catalog identity.
- `C5 reading or phonological candidate`: `blocked` by C4 and by missing
  dated reading history, proposers, alternatives, and negative examples.
- `C6 semantic or grammatical function`: `blocked`. The codepoint label
  `㠱` and any later-form route cannot supply sentence context or grammar.
- `C7 diachronic correspondence`: `blocked`. EvoBC co-membership and a
  shared codepoint do not provide period-provenanced bridge evidence.
- `C8 complete proposition and user delivery`: `withheld`. Rights,
  independent evidence, calibration, leakage controls, and C1--C7
  blockers remain unresolved.

### Concrete next-source questions

1. Which permitted plate, rubbing, or photograph corresponds to each of the
   five catalog-number candidates, and what is its exact locator?
2. Do the five records show the same neighbouring signs, orientation,
   damage pattern, and inscriptional position on the original object?
3. Which catalog or edition verifies `合20217`, `合7896`, `合7897`,
   `合13543`, and `合30173`, rather than only repeating filenames?
4. Which source explains the labels `𠂤組` and `歷無名間`, and who proposed
   those group assignments?
5. Which independent source supplies period, findspot, collection,
   transcription, reading history, and contrary readings?
6. What permission permits a public image derivative or text quotation?

### Alternative and falsification conditions

The current alternatives are: one dataset category contains near-form
mixture; or the apparent differences arise from damage, rubbing, tracing,
cropping, or normalization. The route must be split or marked disputed if
plate-level evidence consistently ties the differences to separate classes.
The split candidate must be withdrawn if multiple views of one object explain
the differences as damage or copying variation.

No reading, meaning, probability, or user-facing AI candidate is estimated.
The object remains `reserved_candidate_not_assigned` and
`unknown_until_cross_source_review`.

- `E1` 是已提交的 HUST 复核派生图。路径、大小、尺寸和 SHA-256

### 命题处置

- `C1 对象身份`：`blocked`。类别和派生图路线稳定，但实物、版本身份和

- `C2 直接字形观察`：E1、E2 为 `direct_checked`。记录下框、竖干、分枝、

  共享大致下部轮廓，但上部结构不同；E2 仍是一家族，尚无独立图版比较或
  拆分规则解除近形风险。
- `C4 卜辞字例与上下文`：`blocked`。`合20217`、`合30173` 等文件名候选

- `C5 读音或隶定候选`：因 C4 以及有日期的释读史、提出者、替代读法和

- `C6 语义或语法功能`：`blocked`。码位标签 `㠱` 和后世字形路线不能替代

- `C7 历时字形对应`：`blocked`。EvoBC 同类和共享码位不构成带时期的桥梁证据。
- `C8 完整命题与用户交付`：`withheld`。权利、独立证据、校准、泄漏控制和

### 替代解释与证伪条件

当前有两种替代解释：一个数据集类别混入了近形；或差异来自损伤、拓摹、
裁切、摹写或归一化。若图版级证据持续把差异对应到不同字类，当前路线必须
拆分或标为争议；若同一对象的多视图证明差异只是损伤或摹写，拆分候选必须撤回。

没有估计释读、意义、概率或面向用户的 AI 候选。本对象仍为
`reserved_candidate_not_assigned` 和 `unknown_until_cross_source_review`。
