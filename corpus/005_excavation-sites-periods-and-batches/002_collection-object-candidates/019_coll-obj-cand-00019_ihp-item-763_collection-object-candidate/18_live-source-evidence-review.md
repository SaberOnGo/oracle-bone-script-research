## Source-reported description and displayed text / 来源说明与显示文字

The English page says the polished antler is carved with the name of a
high-ranking military general during King Wu Ding's reign: Ya and Ch'ueh.
It explains that the Ch'ueh sign combines “small” and “bird,” meaning “small
bird,” and says the combination is used as a personal or state name in oracle
bone inscriptions. It also describes fine carving and a large round eye in
the sparrow depiction.

英文页说打磨光亮的鹿角刻有武丁时期高阶将领“亚”和“雀”的名字。页面解释雀由“小”和
“鸟”组成，字面为“小鸟”，并说组合在甲骨文中用作人名或方国名；页面还说明刻画精细，
雀鸟有圆大眼睛。
