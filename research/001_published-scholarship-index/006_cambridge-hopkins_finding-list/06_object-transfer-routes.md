# Object Transfer Routes / 对象转移路线

## Allowed Candidate Route / 允许的候选路线

A finding-list row may be copied only as source-marked candidate metadata
into a co-located inscription dossier. It must retain the official URL,
access date, period, group, complete row text or audited fields, all available
`c/h/j/y` keys, rights status, and unresolved notes.

finding-list 行只能作为带来源标记的 candidate metadata 转入同一卜辞
对象档案。必须保留官方 URL、访问日期、时期、组别、完整行或已审计
字段、所有可用 `c/h/j/y` 键、权利状态和未解决说明。

## Promotion Gates / 提升门槛

Before assigning a formal `obi-*` record or asserting an identity, obtain:

1. the exact current CUL object record and physical-object identifier;
2. front, reverse, and fragment direction or coverage;
3. the cited Chalfant, *Heji*, and *Yingguo* pages or plates;
4. image, rubbing, transcription, and text-quality evidence;
5. join, duplicate, and renumbering review where a note suggests one;
6. object-level rights and public-derivative permission;
7. an explicit independent review log preserving counterevidence.

在分配正式 `obi-*` 记录或声称对象同一前，必须取得准确馆藏对象号、
正反面和碎片方向、各著录页或图版、图像与释文质量证据、缀合和重号
复核、对象级权利说明，以及保留反证的独立复核日志。

## Prohibited Automatic Transfers / 禁止自动转移

- Do not convert one row into a confirmed object identity.
- Do not convert a shared number into a verified join or duplicate.
- Do not convert a classification heading into an inscription meaning.
- Do not convert the page's note into an accepted reading.
- Do not infer a missing row from the `609`/`612` delta.

- 不把一行自动转成已确认实物身份。
- 不把相同号码自动转成已验证缀合或重复。
- 不把分类标题自动转成卜辞含义。
- 不把网页附注自动转成已接受释读。
- 不从 `609`/`612` 差值推断某一条具体缺失记录。
