## Evidence required before a link / 建立关联前所需证据

1. Bind the rendered response to an exact photographed or rubbed sign.
2. Record its line, neighboring signs, view, scale, and plate locator.
3. Compare variants and near forms without assuming a modern character.
4. Record competing assignments and negative comparisons.
5. Require an independent agent review before emitting a graph edge.

1. 把页面渲染响应绑定到照片或拓片中的准确字形。
2. 记录所在行、相邻字、视图、比例和图版位置。
3. 比较异体和近形，不预设现代汉字对应。
4. 记录竞争性归属和不相合证据。
5. 生成图边前必须经过独立 Agent 复核。

The absence of a graph edge is an evidence decision, not a missing template.
