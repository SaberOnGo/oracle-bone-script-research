# Limits, disputes, and rights / 限制、争议与权利

## Research limits / 研究限制

The museum page is a catalog and display route. It does not establish that
the six short entries are a complete transcription of the photographed
fragments, nor that the two image views are a registered plate assembly.

博物馆页是著录和展示路线，不能证明六条短文本是照片残片的完整释文，也不能证明两条
图像视图已经登记为同一图版拼合。

The object claim gate therefore remains `abstain_withhold_candidate`.
The source text, images, and catalog label are retained as review evidence,
not as a decipherment result.

因此对象主张门仍为 `abstain_withhold_candidate`。来源文字、图像和著录标签只作为复核
证据保留，不是释读结果。

## Rights / 权利

- Effective project status: `metadata_only_until_verified`.
- The two JPEGs remain ignored local inspection files.
- No image, full page, OCR export, or long quotation is redistributed here.
- The museum's photograph permission route must be opened before any public
  derivative is made.

## Dispute and reopen conditions / 争议与重开条件

The record may be reopened only when the exact catalog page or independent
object record is found, the image permission is documented, and a complete
text route can be checked against the images. A named reading must preserve
its proposer and contrary readings.

只有找到具体著录页或独立对象记录、记录图像许可并能把完整文字路线与图像对读后，才可
重开记录。任何释读主张都必须保留提出者和反方释读。

This dossier is not a rights clearance, accepted scholarship, transcription,
or decipherment conclusion.

本档案不是权利许可、已接受学术结论、释文或破译结论。
