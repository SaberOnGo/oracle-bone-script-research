## Direct visual observation / 直接视觉观察

The private high-definition view shows a large broken plastron on a black
background. The upper portion is separated from a larger lower portion. Many
dark incised or painted-looking marks are visible across several fragments;
some areas are pale, abraded, or interrupted by breaks. A small white
fragment and dark handwritten-looking marks are also visible. These are
pixel-level observations only. They do not identify signs, reading order,
plate number, or an inscription sentence.
