## Source-reported description and text / 来源说明与文字

The Chinese page gives five source-reported examples. The following preserves
the displayed wording and punctuation; bracketed or image-substituted signs
remain unresolved source display elements.

中文页给出五条例示。以下保留页面显示的文字和标点；方括号或由图片替代的字仍是来源
页面中的未解决显示元素。

1. `乙酉卜：御新于父戊白豭。`
2. `乙酉卜：御新于匕（妣）辛白盧豕。`
3. `己丑卜：歲父丁、戊[inline source glyph].`
4. `己丑卜：御于帝［卅］小牢。己丑。余至（致）䝅、羊。`
5. `叀小牢于父戊。`

The page's English explanation says examples 1 and 2 concern a person named
Hsin who suffers illness or misfortune and a yu rite, with divinations about
offering white pigs to Wu or Hsin. It describes example 3 as offering a doe
to Ting and Wu, example 4 as offering thirty animals to the Supreme Deity and
boars with goats, and example 5 as an oblation to Wu. These are source-reported
museum explanations, not project translations.

英文说明说例 1、2 涉及名为 Hsin 的人遭遇疾病或祸患及举行御祭，并占问向父戊或妣辛
献白猪；例 3 解释为向父丁、父戊献母羊；例 4 解释为向上帝献三十牲并同日献公猪和羊；
例 5 涉及祭祀父戊。这些是博物馆来源说明，不是本项目翻译。
