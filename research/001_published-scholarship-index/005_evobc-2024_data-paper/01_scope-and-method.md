# Scope And Method / 范围与方法

## Source-Reported Scope / 来源报告范围

The paper reports 229,170 images in 13,714 categories across six dataset
stages. It reports 90,882 images from books and 138,288 from websites.

论文报告六个数据集阶段、229,170 张图和 13,714 个类别；其中 90,882
张来自书籍，138,288 张来自网站。

The six stage labels are OBC, BI, SS, SAC, WSC, and CS. They organize the
dataset. A stage label is not, by itself, archaeological dating evidence for
an individual object.

六个阶段标签为 OBC、BI、SS、SAC、WSC 和 CS。它们用于组织数据集；
阶段标签本身不是单件实物的考古断代证据。

## Source-Reported Processing / 来源报告的加工方法

- Book pages are segmented and grouped using OCR and reading-order steps.
- Website images are treated as already cropped and aligned.
- Labels and backgrounds are normalized for dataset use.
- The paper reports comparison against source books and expert review.
- 书页使用 OCR 和阅读顺序步骤进行切分与分组。
- 网站图像被作为已裁切、已对齐图像处理。
- 标签和背景按数据集用途进行规范化。
- 论文报告了原书对照和专家复核。

These are source reports about dataset production. They do not independently
prove that two images are variants, evolutionary stages, or the same word.

这些是数据集生产过程的来源报告，不独立证明两个图像互为异体、演化阶段
或同一个字。

## Local Material Boundary / 本地材料边界

`Key&Value.json`, `List_of_EVOBC.json`, and `Statistics.xlsx` provide metadata
routes. The registered unified raw image package remains not obtained. Five
downloaded snapshots do not prove possession of that raw package.

`Key&Value.json`、`List_of_EVOBC.json` 和 `Statistics.xlsx` 提供元数据
路线。登记中的统一原始图像包仍未取得；五个已下载快照不能证明已取得
该原包。
