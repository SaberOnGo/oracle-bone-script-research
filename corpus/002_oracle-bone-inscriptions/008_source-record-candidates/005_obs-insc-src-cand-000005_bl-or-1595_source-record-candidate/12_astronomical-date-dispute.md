# Astronomical date dispute / 月食年代争议

## Purpose / 目的

This page puts the named date claims around the Or. 7694/1595 candidate
side by side. It records source scope and evidence status; it does not
choose a date, identify a reading, or confirm that every cited work describes
the same physical object.

本页并列整理 Or. 7694/1595 候选对象周围的有名年代主张。这里只记录来源
范围和证据状态，不选择年代、不确认释读，也不假定所有引文都指同一件实物。

### D-02: 1166 BCE route / D-02：公元前 1166 年路线

- Source: Xueshun Liu, *Early China* 37 (2014), pages 15–38.
- Reported scope: Yingcang 885/886, not an item-level BL catalogue record.
- Reported claim: between 1400 and 1148 BCE, 14 August 1166 BCE is the
  eclipse matching the time pattern and ganzhi dates discussed in the paper.
- Reported method: compare the record with the total eclipse of 16 June 2011
  and use contemporary knowledge of Yin oracle-bone inscriptions.
- Proposers named in the abstract: Liu and Chang Yuzhi had stated this view
  independently in 1998; the article presents another method.
- Evidence level: scholarly article abstract and metadata; full argument,
  plates, and calculations were not copied into this repository.
- Project status: a source-reported scholarly argument, not a project date.

- 来源：刘学顺，*Early China* 37（2014），15–38 页。
- 来源范围：`Yingcang 885/886`，不是大英图书馆逐项记录。
- 来源主张：在公元前 1400 至 1148 年之间，公元前 1166 年 8 月 14 日的
  月食符合论文讨论的时间模式和干支日期。
- 来源方法：与 2011 年 6 月 16 日月全食比较，并使用殷代甲骨文知识。
- 摘要列出的提出者：刘学顺和常玉芝在 1998 年分别提出过该观点；论文
  另以一种方法重新论证。
- 证据等级：论文摘要与书目信息；全文论证、图版和计算未复制到本仓库。
- 项目状态：来源报告的学术论证，不是项目年代。

URL: <https://doi.org/10.1017/eac.2014.10>

## What the disagreement means / 争议的实际含义

The two dates differ by 26 years. They must not be averaged, ranked by model
confidence, or merged into a project field. The present evidence shows a
disagreement between source routes, not a calibrated probability distribution.

两种年代相差 26 年。不能取平均、按模型置信度排序，或合并成项目字段。
当前证据只显示来源路线之间存在分歧，不构成校准概率分布。

The date claims also have different object scopes: D-01 names the BL object
as Yingcang 886, while D-02 reasons over Yingcang 885/886 together. Until the
exact plates and catalog records are matched, neither claim can be silently
transferred to both image sides.

两种主张的对象范围也不同：D-01 把大英图书馆对象标为 Yingcang 886，
D-02 则把 Yingcang 885/886 合并论证。在确切图版和馆藏记录完成对接前，
不能把任一主张静默转给两张图像或两面骨片。

The catalogue warning that the inscription, or part of it, may have been
added in the twentieth century is a separate source-reported risk. It is
neither proof of fabrication nor evidence for either astronomical date.

馆藏目录关于全部或部分刻辞可能在二十世纪添加的警示，是另一条来源报告
风险。它既不是伪造证明，也不是支持任一月食年代的证据。
