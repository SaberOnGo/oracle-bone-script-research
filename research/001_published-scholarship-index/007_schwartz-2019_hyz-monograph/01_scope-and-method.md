# Scope and method / 范围与方法

## What the book covers / 本书范围

The publisher describes the book as an annotated English translation of the
Huayuanzhuang East oracle-bone archive. Its chapter map lists an introduction,
the `HYZ 1` translation section, raw data, parallel content and synchronies,
a bibliography, and an index.

出版社将本书描述为花园庄东甲骨档案的英文注释译本。章节表列出导论、
`HYZ 1` 翻译部分、原始数据、平行内容与同步关系、书目和索引。

The publisher route gives the introduction as pages 1-70 and the `HYZ 1`
section as pages 75-396. These are book-level locators. They do not locate
the cited `HYZ 421` entry without opening a permitted copy.

出版社路线列出导论为第 1-70 页，`HYZ 1` 部分为第 75-396 页。这些是
全书层级定位；在打开获准版本前，不能据此定位 `HYZ 421` 条目。

## Method status / 方法状态

The book is a translation and commentary route. Its readings and translations
must be attributed to Schwartz and checked against the source edition before
the project treats them as evidence for an AI candidate.

本书是翻译和注释路线。书中的释读和翻译必须归于 Schwartz，并与来源整理本
核对后，项目才能把它们作为 AI 候选的证据。

The project has not downloaded the publisher PDF. No local PDF checksum,
OCR, plate crop, or page image is claimed in this dossier.

项目没有下载出版社 PDF。本档案不声称已有本地 PDF checksum、OCR、图版裁图
或页面图像。

## Applicability to HYZ 421 / 对 HYZ 421 的适用范围

The book is potentially applicable because the Commons page gives both the
object reference and the cited pages. The current evidence remains a citation
route only. Museum identity, edition pagination, plate identity, and text
alignment still require separate checks.

本书具有潜在适用性，因为 Commons 页面同时给出对象号和引用页码。当前证据
仍只是引用路线。博物馆身份、版本页码、图版身份和文字对应仍需分别核查。
