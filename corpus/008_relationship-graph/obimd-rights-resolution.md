# OBIMD Graph Rights Resolution / OBIMD 图谱权利解析

## English

This note is the human-first rights route for OBIMD graph consumers. Older
staging rows and graph edges may retain the source-declared
`licensed_for_repository` value. It is historical provenance, not permission
to redistribute an image, package, or derivative.

Resolve the effective status as follows:

1. Open the override ledger:
   `project_registry/004_asset-source-and-rights-index/`
   `006_obimd-rights-status-override.csv`.
2. Open the source-object decision:
   `corpus/006_research-sources-and-bibliography/001_source-objects/`
   `016_src-obimd_source-object/25_effective-rights-decision.md`.
3. Apply `metadata_only_until_verified` and the recorded
   no-redistribution decision to component, variant, asset, and package
   routes.

An edge remains a candidate review route. It is not a rights clearance,
publication approval, component assignment, variant judgment, or decipherment
result.

## 简体中文

本页是 OBIMD 图谱使用者的人类优先权利路线。较早的 staging 行和图边
可能保留来源声明 `licensed_for_repository`。它只是历史出处字段，不是
复制图像、来源包或派生件的许可。

解析当前状态时：

1. 打开覆盖表：
   `project_registry/004_asset-source-and-rights-index/`
   `006_obimd-rights-status-override.csv`。
2. 打开来源对象决定页：
   `corpus/006_research-sources-and-bibliography/001_source-objects/`
   `016_src-obimd_source-object/25_effective-rights-decision.md`。
3. 对构件、异体、资产和来源包路线统一使用
   `metadata_only_until_verified`及记录中的不得再分发决定。

图边仍只是候选复核路线，不是权利清理、公开批准、构件归属、异体判断
或破译结果。
