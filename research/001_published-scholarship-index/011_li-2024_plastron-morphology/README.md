## Claim and page locator / 说法与页码定位

Printed page 36 distinguishes two forms of the original edge of the throat
shield: flat-cut and blunt-rounded. Footnote 2 lists `Huadong 421` among six
examples of the blunt-rounded form, together with 124, 420, 426, 452, and
480.

书面第 36 页把喉盾原边分为平截、钝圆两类。脚注 2 把《花东》421 与 124、
420、426、452、480 一并列为钝圆形实例。

This is an independent published morphology locator for HYZ 421. It is not
a plate of HYZ 421 and contains no measurement or museum accession record for
the Ningxia photograph.

这是 HYZ 421 的独立已发表形态定位，但不是 HYZ 421 图版，也没有提供宁夏
照片的尺寸或馆藏号。

## Applicability to the photograph / 对照片的适用范围

The committed photograph shows a curved upper contour rather than an obvious
straight truncation. That observation is compatible with the source-reported
blunt-rounded class, but it is not diagnostic:

已提交照片的上缘呈曲线，没有明显平截。该观察与来源报告的钝圆类别相容，
但不具排他性：

- the article names five other Huadong objects in the same class;
- the photograph does not isolate the anatomical throat-shield edge;
- restoration, orientation, and scale are not independently controlled;
- the 21.6 x 15.1 versus 28.3 x 20 cm conflict remains unexplained.

- 论文还列出五件同类花东对象；
- 照片没有单独标出解剖学上的喉盾原边；
- 修复、方向和比例均未独立控制；
- 21.6 × 15.1 与 28.3 × 20 厘米冲突仍未解释。

## Falsification use / 反证用途

If a source-edition plate shows HYZ 421 with a flat-cut rather than
blunt-rounded throat shield, either the article locator or the plate mapping
must be rechecked. If the Ningxia photograph is later shown to have a
flat-cut throat shield, its HYZ 421 identification loses another support.

如果底本图版显示 HYZ 421 的喉盾原边是平截而非钝圆，就必须重新核查论文
定位或图版对应。如果以后证明宁夏照片的喉盾原边为平截，其 HYZ 421 身份将
再失去一项支持。
