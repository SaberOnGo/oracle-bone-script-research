## Source-reported description / 来源说明

The English page labels the object a significant antiquity and says that the
practice inscriptions include lively depictions of a tiger, two monkeys, a
horse above a burning fire, and a bird with an arrow above its head, similar
to the character “pheasant.” It says the images are valuable for Chinese art
history and links to an IHP 3D gallery.

英文页把对象标为重要古物，并说习刻包含一只虎、两只猴、火上之马，以及头上有箭、形似
“雉”字的一只鸟。页面说这些生动形象对中国美术史有价值，并链接到史语所 3D 展示。
