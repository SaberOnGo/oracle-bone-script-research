# Claim Gate / 主张门槛

## Allowed claims now / 当前允许主张

- Official item pages, object labels, response checksums, and dimensions exist.
- The page displays three fragmentary lines and three rendered glyph responses.
- The two large responses permit bounded visual observations.
- Rights remain `metadata_only_until_verified`.

- 官方对象页面、对象标签、响应校验和与尺寸已经记录。
- 页面显示三行残缺文字和三份页面渲染字形响应。
- 两份大图只允许有限视觉观察。
- 权利状态仍为 `metadata_only_until_verified`。

## Withheld claims / 暂缓主张

Formal `obi-*` identity, complete transcription, translation, personal name,
genealogy, join sequence, period group, character identity, and decipherment
are withheld. Delivery state is `abstain_withhold_candidate`.

正式 `obi-*` 身份、完整释文、翻译、人名、谱系、拼合次序、时期组类、
单字身份和破译均暂缓。交付状态为 `abstain_withhold_candidate`。

## Promotion gate / 晋级门槛

Promotion requires independent catalog and plate locators, image-to-line and
image-to-sign mappings, a sourced transcription history, recorded competing
views, rights review, and independent Agent adjudication. A high model score
without these evidence bindings is not enough.

晋级需要独立著录与图版位置、图像到行和字形的映射、有来源的释读史、
不同意见、权利复核和独立 Agent 裁决。缺少这些证据绑定时，高模型分数
也不足以晋级。
