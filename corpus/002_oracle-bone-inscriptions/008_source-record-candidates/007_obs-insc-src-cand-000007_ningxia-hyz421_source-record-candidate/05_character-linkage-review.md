# Character linkage review / 单字关联复核

## Current state / 当前状态

No character occurrence is assigned to this source-record candidate. The
displayed string is not split into confirmed graphs, and no Unicode or modern
character mapping is stored as a project conclusion.

本来源记录候选尚未分配任何单字出现。页面字符串没有拆分成已确认字形，
也没有把 Unicode 或今字对应写成项目结论。

## Permitted future route / 允许的后续路线

After a permitted plate or edition is opened, a researcher may record one
candidate occurrence at a time with its image region, source locator, edition
form, damage state, and reviewer note. Any link to an existing character must
remain a candidate until the image, source text, and cross-source evidence
agree.

取得获准图版或版本后，研究者可以逐项记录候选字形出现、图像区域、来源定位、
版本字形、残损状态和复核说明。只有图像、来源文字和跨源证据一致后，才能考虑
与已有单字建立候选关系；在此之前都保持候选状态。

## Missing checks / 缺失检查

- graph boundaries on the photograph;
- relation between the two reported divinations;
- source edition and page or plate;
- damaged or editorially supplied graphs;
- comparison with other HYZ 421 records.

- 照片中的字形边界；
- 两条来源卜辞之间的关系；
- 来源版本及页码或图版；
- 残损字和编者补字；
- 与 HYZ 421 其他记录的比较。
