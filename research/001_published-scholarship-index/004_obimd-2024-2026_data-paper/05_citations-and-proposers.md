# Citations And Proposers / 引用与提出者

## Dataset And Platform Routes / 数据与平台路线

- Hugging Face record: https://doi.org/10.57967/hf/7828
- Code: https://github.com/libang1991/OBIMD
- Annotation platform: https://www.jgwlbq.org.cn/oracle-bone
- YinQiWenYuan supplies the digital material route.
- *Jiaguwen Heji* and Huayuanzhuang East publications supply core rubbing
  source claims.
- The Oracular Digital Platform supplies the Jingyuan character hierarchy.

These relations are citations and provenance routes. They do not make an
OBIMD label an accepted reading or make two catalog IDs the same object.

这些关系是引用和出处路线，不会使 OBIMD 标签成为已接受释读，也不证明两
个著录号指向同一实物。

## Citation Questions / 引用待查

- Which edition, volume, page, and plate support each `RubbingName`?
- Which transcription publication and page supplied each reading sequence?
- Which Jingyuan UID revision supplied each `Label` and `SubLabel`?
- Which cited source records disagreement with the provided transcription?
- 每个 `RubbingName` 对应哪一版、哪一册、哪一页和哪一图版？
- 每条阅读顺序来自哪种释文出版物和哪一页？
- 每个 `Label`、`SubLabel` 来自哪个 Jingyuan UID 版本？
- 哪项被引资料记录了与所给释文不同的意见？
