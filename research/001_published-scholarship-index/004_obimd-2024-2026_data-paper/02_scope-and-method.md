# Scope And Method / 范围与方法

## Material Scope / 资料范围

The journal article describes a three-level corpus linking full rubbing and
facsimile images, sentence or non-sentential groups, and character boxes. It
also provides transcriptions and within-group reading orders.

期刊论文描述三级语料：完整拓片与摹本图像、句组或非句组、单字框，并提供
释文和组内阅读顺序。

Rubbing, facsimile, transcription, character category, and reading sequence
are separate evidence layers. Pixel alignment does not make them equivalent.

拓片、摹本、释文、字类和阅读顺序是不同证据层；像素对齐不使其等同。

## Journal Workflow / 期刊版流程

The current article locates the workflow under `Methods`:

1. `Data acquisition`: gathers rubbings through YinQiWenYuan, aligns
   facsimiles, and exposes transcription-page references.
2. `Pre-annotation`: algorithms propose boxes and top-10 shape matches.
3. Collaborative annotation: non-specialists refine preliminary records.
4. Verification: trained graduates cross-check; experts review remaining
   errors and unresolved cases.

当前论文在 `Methods` 下说明：

1. `Data acquisition`：经殷契文渊取得拓片，并对齐摹本、提供释文页参考。
2. `Pre-annotation`：算法提出字框和形近前十候选。
3. 协作标注：非专业标注者完善初步记录。
4. 复核：受训研究生交叉核查，专家复核剩余错误和未决案例。

All review and efficiency statements remain `author-reported`. The project
has not opened a per-box adjudication history for all 115,319 boxes.

所有复核和效率说法均为 `author-reported`。本项目尚未取得 115,319 个字框
逐框裁决史。

## Source Layers / 来源层

- 9,913 rubbing records are reported from *Jiaguwen Heji*.
- 164 are reported from Huayuanzhuang East material.
- Facsimiles and transcription pages come through published compilations and
  the YinQiWenYuan route.
- Character hierarchy follows the Jingyuan library on the Oracular Digital
  Platform.

Each layer needs edition, catalog, page, image, and rights review before an
object dossier promotes it beyond a candidate route.

各层进入对象档案并超越候选路线前，都须复核版本、著录、页码、图像和权利。
