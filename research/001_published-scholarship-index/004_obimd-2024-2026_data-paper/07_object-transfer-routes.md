# Object Transfer Routes / 对象转移路线

## Inscription Dossier Route / 卜辞档案路线

An OBIMD entry may supply a candidate route for:

- rubbing abbreviation and image member;
- facsimile member and alignment relation;
- source-reported transcription and text group;
- character boxes, order, missing positions, and exception codes;
- reported source publication family.

OBIMD 条目可提供下列候选路线：

- 拓片简称和图像成员；
- 摹本成员及对齐关系；
- 来源报告的释文和文本组；
- 字框、顺序、缺字位置和例外代码；
- 论文报告的来源出版物族。

Every relation remains a `candidate route` until the catalog, image,
transcription page, rights statement, and conflicting scholarship are opened.

在打开著录、图像、释文页、权利声明和相反学术意见前，每项关系均保持
`candidate route`。

## Character And Component Route / 单字与构件路线

Transfer `Label`, `SubLabel`, UID, and modern lookup character separately.
Do not turn the hierarchy into a component claim, variant decision, Unicode
identity, or accepted modern reading.

分别转移 `Label`、`SubLabel`、UID 和今字检索标签；不得把该层级改写成
构件结论、异体判断、Unicode 身份或获接受的今字释读。

## Exception Transfer / 例外转移

- Preserve `SeatFont` and `Mark` exactly with the source version.
- Open both rubbing and facsimile before interpreting `Mark = 2` or `3`.
- Keep `Mark = 1` as an unresolved dispute.
- Do not infer a missing glyph identity from a `SeatFont` placeholder.
- 按来源版本原样保存 `SeatFont` 和 `Mark`。
- 解释 `Mark = 2` 或 `3` 前同时打开拓片和摹本。
- `Mark = 1` 保持为未决争议。
- 不得从 `SeatFont` 占位推断缺字身份。

## Promotion Gate / 提升门槛

Promotion requires exact source publication, page or plate, catalog number,
opened images, text-quality review, rights scope, and disagreement search.
Author-reported expert review alone is insufficient.

提升须具备确切来源出版物、页码或图版、著录号、已打开图像、文本质量
复核、权利范围和异议检索。仅有作者报告的专家复核仍不足。
