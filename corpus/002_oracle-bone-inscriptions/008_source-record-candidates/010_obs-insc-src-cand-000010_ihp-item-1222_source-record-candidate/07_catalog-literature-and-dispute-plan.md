## Literature questions / 文献问题

- Who first proposed the current join, and on what visual evidence?
- Which publication first supplied each displayed line and rendered sign?
- Who proposed the repeated-name or genealogy interpretation?
- Which scholars reject, modify, or leave that interpretation unresolved?
- Are there alternative transcriptions for the boxes and damaged regions?

- 现有拼合由谁首先提出，视觉依据是什么？
- 每行页面文字和渲染字形最早见于哪一出版物？
- 重复人名或谱系解释由谁提出？
- 哪些研究者反对、修改或保留该解释？
- 方框与残损区域是否有不同释文？

Until these routes are cited and compared, the dossier records a dispute
search plan, not a reading history.
