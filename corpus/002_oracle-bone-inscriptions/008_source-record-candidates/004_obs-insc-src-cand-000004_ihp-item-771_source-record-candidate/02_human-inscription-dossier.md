# Human inscription source dossier / 人类卜辞来源档案

## Source-reported wording / 来源文字

The official English page gives this proposed complete divination:

> “On the day *ting-wei*, diviner '永 Yung' questioned: will it rain from
> today until the day *hsin-hai”*.

The page also says that the preface naming the day and diviner was engraved
on the reverse. The quotation and reverse-side statement are source-reported
English, not a project transcription or translation.

馆方英文页面给出一段完整占问的拟译，并说明标明日期和占人姓名的前辞刻在
背面。这里保留来源英文和来源说法，不建立项目摹写或译文。

The captured page renders one personal-name character ambiguously. It remains
an unresolved source-rendering issue; this project does not normalize it.
