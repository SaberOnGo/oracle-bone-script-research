## Source-reported description and displayed text / 来源说明与显示文字

The English page says the carapace records divinations asking whether a god
had brought harm against the state. It describes the reshaped oval carapace,
its central hole, and the page's claim that this remodeling type is exclusive
to Pit YH127. It also describes a two-snake depiction used as a god's name,
one related sign explained through “toe” and “snake,” and another snake form.

英文页说此背甲记录占问神祇是否降灾于我方，并说明背甲被改制为中孔椭圆形，页面称此类
改制背甲为 YH127 坑所独有。页面还说明一字像两条蛇并作神祇名，另一相关字以“虫”和
“止”及蛇咬脚趾的说明解释，另有一种较宽且有纹饰的蛇形字。

The Chinese page displays source text including `䖵（害）我。` and
`䖵不我（害）。` The sign shown as `䖵` and the parenthetical reading are
source-page content. They are not independently checked by this project.

中文页显示包括 `䖵（害）我。` 和 `䖵不我（害）。` 在内的来源文字。`䖵` 及括号中的
释读属于来源页面内容，本项目尚未独立核验。
