# Limits, disputes, and rights / 限制、争议与权利

## Publication metadata / 出版信息

The publisher route displays a 2019 eBook date and a 2020 copyright line;
the university record cites 2019. This is a bibliographic-display issue,
not a basis for choosing a project dating of the object or inscription.

出版社路线显示 2019 年电子书日期和 2020 年版权行；大学记录引用 2019 年。
这是书目显示差异，不能据此选择项目对象或卜辞年代。

## Page and text limits / 页码与文字限制

The project checked the HYZ 421 entry and raw-data row. The edition has two
numbered entries where Commons displays one string. The source plate and
graph-level alternatives remain unopened, so the project does not normalize
the uncertain forms.

项目已检查 HYZ 421 条目和原始数据行。版本有两条编号记录，Commons 只显示
一条字符串。底本图版和字形层面的异说仍未打开，所以项目不规范存疑字形。

The edition gives 21.6 by 15.1 cm, while Commons gives 28.3 by 20 cm. This
is an object-identity dispute, not a rounding difference. The photograph is
not accepted as HYZ 421 until independent evidence explains it.

版本尺寸为 21.6 × 15.1 厘米，Commons 为 28.3 × 20 厘米。这是对象身份争议，
不是四舍五入差异。在独立证据解释前，不接受该照片就是 HYZ 421。

## Rights / 权利

The publisher page reports `CC BY-NC-ND 3.0`; the PDF states
`CC BY-NC-ND 4.0`. Both prohibit derivatives. The mismatch is retained and
no PDF pages, OCR, or extracted translations are committed.

出版社页面报告 `CC BY-NC-ND 3.0`，PDF 内页写 `CC BY-NC-ND 4.0`。两者均
禁止演绎。本项目保留差异，不提交 PDF 页面、OCR 或抽取译文。

No raw PDF or extracted book text is committed. The inspected access copy is

`9e53e314082a992231ed0c48bbad35ac`; it remains in ignored storage.
