# Text quality review / 文字质量复核

## Available layers / 已有层级

- Image: one public Commons route, locally checksum-checked.
- Source title: available as page metadata.
- Accession and catalog hints: source-displayed, not independently matched.
- Full text: not available in the checked snapshot.
- OCR: not available in the checked snapshot.
- Project transcription: deliberately not created.
- Translation or reading: deliberately not created.

## Concrete quality limits / 具体质量限制

The current image does not provide a line-addressable text edition. A
researcher must locate the British Library object record and a catalog or
plate publication that identifies the exact page, plate, image side, and
line order. The next text pass must compare any transcription against the
image and record the edition, page, plate, and reviewer.

当前不能把图像刻痕直接转成 OCR 或释文。待查问题是：哪个权威著录的
哪一页或哪一版提供逐行文字？其图版是否明确对应 `1535v`？是否有
异体、残缺和争议标记？这些问题解决前，文字状态仍是候选路线。
