## Counterevidence / 反证

The inline responses have clean black strokes on white fields and lack bone
texture. This is counterevidence against treating them as direct bone-surface
crops. Seams and repairs are also counterevidence against inferring an
original sequence from the current assembled outline alone.

内嵌响应是白底黑线且没有骨面纹理，这是反对把它们当作骨面直接裁切图的
证据。拼缝与修补也反对仅凭当前拼合外形推断原始次序。

No visual observation in this file supplies a reading or decipherment.

本页任何视觉观察都不构成释读或破译。
