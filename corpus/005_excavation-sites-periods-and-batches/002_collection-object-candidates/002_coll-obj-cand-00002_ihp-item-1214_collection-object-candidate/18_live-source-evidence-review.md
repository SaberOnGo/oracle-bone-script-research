## Text quality and provenance / 文本质量与来源

The museum page gives a short source display with image placeholders and a
short English description. It does not provide an item-level publication page,
complete OCR, plate locator, or independent scholarly citation in the current
route. The text status is therefore `source_reported_partial_text`.

博物馆页给出带图像占位的短来源展示和英文说明，但当前路由没有提供对象级出版物页、
完整 OCR、图版定位或独立学术引文。因此文本状态为
`source_reported_partial_text`。

The object page, image URLs, local JPEG checksums, and future HTML capture must
remain separate provenance records. They do not by themselves prove that all
