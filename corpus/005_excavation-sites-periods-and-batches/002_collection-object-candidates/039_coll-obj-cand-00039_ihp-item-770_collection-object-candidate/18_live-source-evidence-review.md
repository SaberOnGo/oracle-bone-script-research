## Source description / 来源描述

The English page reports ink remains from the Shang Dynasty and says that
the divination asked whether rain would come on the next day, identified as
the day *ping*. This is a source-reported description, not a new translation.

英文页面称其保留商代墨迹，并描述一条询问次日是否下雨、日期为 *ping*
日的占问。这是来源原述，不是本项目新增翻译，也不等于已恢复完整卜辞。

### Direct visual observations / 直接视觉观察

The high-definition view shows a large irregular plastron fragment on a white
background. The surface is porous and uneven, with broken edges, dark linear
marks, broad ochre and brown areas, and red-brown coloration across parts of
the central and right surface. A pale fragment projects from the lower left.

高清图显示白底上的大型不规则龟甲腹甲残片。表面多孔且不平整，边缘破损，
可见深色线状痕迹；中部和右侧有大片赭色、棕色及红棕色区域；左下方有一块
浅色外伸残片。

These observations describe pixels only. They do not identify a sign, reading,
stroke order, cinnabar layer, original orientation, or relation between marks.

以上只描述图像可见像素，不确认字形、释读、笔顺、朱砂层、原始方向或线痕
之间的关系。
