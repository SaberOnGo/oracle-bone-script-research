## Reading history and dispute / 释读史与争议

The 2024 source presents a revised transcription and argues for shallow
practice cuts, erased traces, repeated personal names, and a
genealogy-like record. It also distinguishes this structure from a strict
genealogy and recommends the more cautious label “genealogy-like”.

2024 年来源提出修订释文，并主张存在浅刻习刻、刮除旧迹、连续私名和
类谱系记录。文章同时承认其结构不同于严格家谱，并建议使用较谨慎的
“类家谱刻辞”名称。

The source reports a long authenticity dispute. It names Hu Houxuan and
Yu Xingwu's opposed 1980 articles as representative positions. It also
records later support, doubt, and disagreement about the wording commonly
rendered as `zi yue`. These are history-of-scholarship records, not votes
converted into a project probability.

该来源报告了长期真伪争议，并以胡厚宣、于省吾 1980 年两篇立场相反的
论文为代表。它还记录后续支持、怀疑，以及对通常写作 `zi yue` 句式的
分歧。这些只是学术史记录，不能把人数换算成本项目概率。

The source names Jin Xiangheng, Yan Yiping, Matsumaru Michio,
Chen Weizhan, Song Zhenhao, and Cai Zhemao among doubters. It names
Yang Shengnan, Zhang Bingquan, Wang Yuxin, Wang Tao, and Huang Guohui
among supporters. It also reports that Allan and Chen Guangyu's instrument
work and Cao Dingyun's response reached opposed implications.

来源把金祥恒、严一萍、松丸道雄、陈炜湛、宋镇豪、蔡哲茂列入
怀疑者，把杨升南、张秉权、王宇信、汪涛、黄国辉列入支持者；又报告
艾兰、陈光宇的仪器观察与曹定云的回应导向相反含义。

Bibliographic disagreement is preserved. The 2025 NCCU thesis bibliography
gives Zhang and Song's 2018 article as pages `20-29`; the 2024 repost's
footnote gives pages `20-30`. The original printed volume must decide it.

书目差异原样保留。政大 2025 年论文书目把张惟捷、宋雅萍 2018 年论文
记作第 `20-29` 页；2024 年转载页脚注记作第 `20-30` 页。
最终页码须由原书核定。

## Counterevidence and limits / 反证与限制

The strongest counterevidence is methodological. The 2024 source says the
erased shallow traces require repeated physical inspection under strong
light and changing angles. It says rubbings and database photographs are
insufficient for a reliable reading.

最强反证来自方法本身。2024 年来源明确说，刮除后的浅刻痕必须在强光下
反复调整角度实物目验；拓片和数据库照片不足以可靠释读。

This project has two museum JPEG views and three rendered glyph responses.
They cannot reproduce that physical inspection. Therefore the article's
trace readings, A-D seams, writing rotations, and genealogy-like analysis
remain source reports. This is both `counterevidence` and a hard claim gate.

本项目只有两幅馆方 JPEG 和三个页面渲染字形响应，不能替代实物目验。
因此，论文的字痕释读、A-D 接缝、转向书写和类谱系分析仍是来源报告。
这既是 `反证`，也是硬性主张门槛。
