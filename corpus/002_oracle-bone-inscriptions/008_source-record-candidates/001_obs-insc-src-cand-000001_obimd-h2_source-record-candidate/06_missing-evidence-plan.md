# Missing Evidence And Next Checks / 缺失证据与后续核查

## English

This dossier is reproducible at the OBIMD package level but not complete at
the archaeological or catalog level. The next checks are concrete:

The Heji-number question now has an uncalibrated candidate route to Heji 2.
Volume 1, first period, plate 2 and the older routes `粹866`, `善9025`, and
`北圖` are source-reported. The printed leaf or page is still unopened.
OBIMD is directly derived from Yinqi Wenyuan. National Library,
Xiaoxuetang, and Liu 2011 share the Heji catalog, image, or text tradition;
they must not be counted as independent paleographic readings.

1. Open Heji volume 1, first period, plate 2, identify its printed leaf, and
   compare its object against the National Library and OBIMD images.
2. Identify the edition, volume, plate, and page from which the rubbing and
   reference transcription were drawn. Preserve page-level evidence.
3. Establish the collection institution and object number from a catalog or
   museum record with independently documented object provenance. Record its
   ancestry and do not infer fields from the file name.
4. Find the findspot or excavation context, including pit or batch when a
   cited archaeological record supplies it.
5. Test Liu 2011's Bin-group assignment against a second named classification
   system and record disagreements rather than selecting one silently.
6. Locate an edition-level Heji 2 transcription. Compare its editor, page,
   missing signs, and disputed positions with the modern lookup routes and
   the seventh route's PUA / multi-character-reference granularity warning.
7. Resolve the OBIMD rights conflict before publishing any image derivative.

## Stop Conditions / 停止条件

Do not assign a formal inscription ID merely because a number resembles a
catalog number. Do not fill a missing field with a probable value. Record a
failed search route, access date, and source checked when evidence is absent.

不得因为编号看似著录号就分配正式卜辞 ID，不得用可能值填补缺失字段。
找不到证据时，应记录查过的来源、访问日期和失败路线。
