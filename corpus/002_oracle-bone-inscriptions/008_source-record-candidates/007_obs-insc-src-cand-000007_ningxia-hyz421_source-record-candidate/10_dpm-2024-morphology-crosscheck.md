## Object-level result / 对象层结果

Li 2024 printed page 36 lists `Huadong 421` as an example of a
blunt-rounded original edge of the throat shield. The Ningxia photograph's
visible upper contour is curved and does not show an obvious flat truncation.

李延彦 2024 书面第 36 页把《花东》421 列为喉盾原边钝圆的实例。宁夏照片
可见上缘为曲线，没有明显平截。

This is compatible but weak evidence. The article puts five other Huadong
objects in the same class, and the photograph does not isolate the relevant
anatomical edge. Morphology therefore cannot choose between HYZ 421 and other
same-class objects or explain the dimension conflict.

这是相容但较弱的证据。论文还把五件花东对象列入同类，照片也没有单独标出
相关解剖边缘。因此，形态不能在 HYZ 421 与其他同类对象之间作出选择，也不能
解释尺寸冲突。
