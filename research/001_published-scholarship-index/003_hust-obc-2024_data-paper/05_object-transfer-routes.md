# Object Transfer Routes / 对象转移路线

## Transfer Contract / 转移约束

The paper may supply a candidate route to a character dossier. It cannot by
itself supply an accepted identity or reading. Every transfer must preserve
the paper, package member, source-family code, rights note, and review status.

论文只能向单字档案提供候选路线，不能单独提供获接受的身份或释读。每次
转移都须保留论文、包内成员、来源族代码、权利说明和复核状态。

## Character Dossier Route / 单字档案路线

Transfer only these source facts before original-source review:

- HUST category and deciphered or undeciphered split;
- package member path, file name, checksum route, and image type;
- source-reported modern label as a candidate route;
- known missing context and the exact next source to open.

在原始来源复核前，只能转移：

- HUST 类别及已释或未释分区；
- 包内路径、文件名、校验和路线和图像类型；
- 标为候选路线的来源报告今字标签；
- 已知背景缺失和下一项须打开的确切来源。

## Required Evidence Before Promotion / 提升前必需证据

- Open the original book page, website record, or database object.
- Record plate, catalog, inscription, neighboring signs, and image relation.
- Compare at least one independent source family where available.
- Review the third-party rights statement for the exact image.
- Record disagreement and abstain when evidence remains insufficient.
- 打开原始书页、网页记录或数据库对象。
- 记录图版、著录、卜辞、邻字及图像关系。
- 在可能时比较至少一个独立来源族。
- 复核该具体图像的第三方权利说明。
- 证据不足时记录分歧并弃权。

## Prohibited Transfers / 禁止转移

- 94.6% classifier accuracy as a reading probability.
- A HUST category as proof that two forms are variants.
- An `undeciphered` folder number as an archaeological identifier.
- A GuoXueDaShi item without the explicit reliability caution.
- 不能把 94.6% 分类准确率转移为释读概率。
- 不能把 HUST 类别当作两字形互为异体的证明。
- 不能把未释目录号当作考古对象编号。
- 不能在省略可靠性风险时转移 GuoXueDaShi 条目。

All transferred relations remain a `candidate route` until reviewed.
所有转移关系在复核前均保持 `candidate route`。
