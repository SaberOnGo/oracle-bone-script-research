## Falsification And Reopen Conditions / 证伪与重开条件

- Withdraw the object-to-plate route if an independent edition gives a
  different item, accession, period, or plate mapping.
- Withdraw the opposing-surface relation if catalog evidence shows E2 and E3
  are different objects, repairs, or unrelated image routes.
- Withdraw any text alignment if a cited plate or rubbing places the six
  entries or placeholders differently from the museum page.
- Reopen C5--C7 only after a cited plate locator, line-addressable text,
  exact glyph positions, and a dated disagreement or reading-history route
  are recorded.

- 如果独立版本把对象、登记号、时期或图版对应到其他记录，撤回对象—图版路线。
- 如果著录证明 E2 与 E3 属于不同对象、修补处或无关图像路线，撤回相对表面关联。

- 只有记录带出处的图版定位、可定位文字、精确字位和有日期的争议或释读史
