The high-definition view shows a pale plastron on a white background. Broad
cracks divide the surface into irregular areas. Dark linear marks, repeated
short strokes, rectangular and curved forms, and red or brown pigment traces
are visible. These are pixel observations only; they do not identify signs,
establish reading order, or prove that every line is writing.
