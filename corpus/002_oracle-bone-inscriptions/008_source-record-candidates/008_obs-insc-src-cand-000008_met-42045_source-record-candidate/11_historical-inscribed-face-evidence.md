## Direct visual record / 直接视觉记录

The photograph shows a long, narrow, gently curved bone against a dark
background. The object is placed horizontally. Its two ends differ in width
and outline, and the upper and lower long edges are not parallel.

照片显示深色背景上的狭长、微弯骨片，实物横向放置。两端宽度和轮廓不同，
上下长边也不平行。

Fine incised marks are visible across much of the pale surface. Their contrast
is stronger than in the current color `primaryImage`. Several surface-spanning
linear boundaries or changes are also visible along the long axis. This page
does not decide whether any such line is a crack, join, preparation mark,
inscription delimiter, photographic artifact, or later damage.

浅色骨面的大部分范围可见细刻痕，其反差强于当前彩色 `primaryImage`。
沿长轴还可见若干跨越骨面的线性边界或变化。本页不判断这些线属于裂缝、
缀合、整治痕、刻辞分界、摄影现象还是后期损伤。

The image resolution and compression do not support a reliable sign count.
No visible cluster is assigned a modern character, component, sound, meaning,
or grammatical role.

图像分辨率和压缩程度不足以支持可靠字数统计。没有把任何可见刻痕组合分配
给今字、构件、读音、意义或语法功能。

## Counterevidence and alternatives / 反证与替代解释

- The older file may be a resized or contrast-altered publication derivative.
- Its dark background and horizontal presentation change the apparent shape.
- A matching accession does not prove that every historical file label is
  error-free.
- Similar object outline does not establish line-by-line inscription identity.
- No independent catalog, plate, or conservation record was found in this
  search round.

- 早期文件可能是缩放或调整反差后的出版派生图。
- 深色背景和横向放置改变了表观轮廓。
- 馆藏号相同不能证明每条历史文件标签都没有错误。
- 实物轮廓相似不能建立逐行刻辞同一性。
- 本轮没有找到独立著录、图版或保护记录。

## Falsification conditions / 证伪条件

Withdraw the surface candidate if a Met record or catalog assigns the older
file to another accession, another object assembly, or a different view than
the current inscribed surface. Reopen it if a catalog plate supplies a face
label and matching damage or inscription landmarks.

如果 Met 记录或著录把早期文件分配给另一馆藏号、另一实物组合，或证明它
不是当前刻辞面，应撤回图面候选。若著录图版给出面标，并能对应损伤点或
刻辞定位点，则重新复核并提升证据等级。
