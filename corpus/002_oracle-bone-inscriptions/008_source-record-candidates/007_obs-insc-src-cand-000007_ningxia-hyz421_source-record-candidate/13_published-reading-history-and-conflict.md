# Published reading history and conflict
# 公开释读史与冲突

## Result / 结果

A 2013 peer-reviewed article reports that `H3:421` contains an activity
described as avoiding rain at a place. The article attributes that statement
to Han Jiangsu's 2007 book, printed pages 351--353. This conflicts with the
current Schwartz route, which records two repeated entries about ritual food
from one ewe in Rong.

2013 年一篇同行评议论文报告：`H3:421` 含有在某地避雨的活动。该文把
这项说法追溯到韩江苏 2007 年专著第 351--353 页。这与当前 Schwartz 路线
形成冲突；后者记录两条重复辞例，内容涉及在戎用一只母羊制作祭祀食物。

This is a high-value conflict candidate. It is not a new project reading.
Before Han's pages and the 2003 source edition are checked, the project must
not decide whether one source omitted text, used a different numbering system,
or made an error.

这是高价值冲突候选，不是本项目的新释读。在核对韩江苏原书和 2003 年底本
以前，项目不能判断究竟是来源漏录、编号体系不同，还是某处存在错误。

Schwartz also lists HYZ 421 among ritual activity at Rong on printed p. 43.
On printed pp. 49--50, he retains competing geographical routes and warns
that similar graphs may write different words. Those cautions remain
counterevidence against treating `Rong` as a settled place identity.

Schwartz 在书面第 43 页把 HYZ 421 列入戎地祭祀活动；书面第 49--50 页

不能把“戎”的地点身份写成定论。

## Related `yi` dispute / 相关“宜”字争议

Zheng Banghong's 2012 note summarizes a wider disagreement. Yao Xuan treats
`yi` as offering viands, while Cao Dingyun and Han Jiangsu treat it as a
sacrifice name. Zheng also treats it as a sacrifice name but gives it a
benefactive construction in another inscription.

郑邦宏 2012 年文章整理了更广的分歧：姚萱把“宜”理解为进献肴肉，曹定云、
韩江苏把它视为祭名；郑邦宏也认作祭名，但在另一辞例中采用为动结构。

Review route / 复核路线:
<https://www.fdgwz.org.cn/Web/Show/1770>

That article is not a direct reading of HYZ 421. It only prevents the English
wording in Schwartz from being treated as an undisputed semantic conclusion.

该文不是对 HYZ 421 的直接释读。它只说明不能把 Schwartz 的英文措辞当作
没有争议的语义结论。

## Falsifiers and next checks / 证伪条件与下一步

The conflict is resolved or rejected only if a checked source shows one of
these outcomes:

只有来源复核出现以下结果之一，才能解决或否定这项冲突：

1. Han 2007, pp. 351--353 uses another object number or corrects `421`.
2. The 2003 plate and transcription contain an additional rain-related line.
3. The numbering used by Shin and Han differs from Schwartz's `HYZ 421`.
4. A published correction identifies a typographical or citation error.

Until then, C4 remains `candidate_route`, C5 and C6 remain `blocked`, and C8
remains `abstain_withhold`. No numeric probability is displayed.

在此之前，C4 仍为 `candidate_route`，C5、C6 仍为 `blocked`，C8 仍为
`abstain_withhold`。本档案不显示数值概率。
