English:
The upper area has a narrow pointed or triangular trace with a light interior.
A central upright stroke descends from it. Several short strokes branch to the
right of the lower center, while a further trace continues downward. The sides
remain uneven and open against the background.

中文：
上部有一条尖状或三角状痕迹，内部保持明亮。中心有竖向痕迹从其下方延伸。
下部中心右侧有几条短痕迹分出，另有痕迹继续向下。两侧边缘不均匀，并在背
景上保持开放。
