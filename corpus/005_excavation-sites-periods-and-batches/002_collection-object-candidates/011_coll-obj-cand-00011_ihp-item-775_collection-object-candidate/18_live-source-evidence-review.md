## Source-reported page description / 来源页面说明

The English page says that the displayed object records divinations about
capturing deer, elaphures, and wild buffalo, and also displays offering
records involving a dog, goat, and person. It says the reverse records a
cattle offering and the acquisition of 451 elaphures. These are museum
descriptions, not project conclusions.

中文页面说明正面记录猎获鹿、麋及兕的占问，并有犬、羊、人等献祭记录；
背面记录献祭一头牛和获麋 451 头。以上均为博物馆页面说明，不是本项目
结论。
