# Visual region review / 图像区域复核

## Purpose / 目的

This page records a first-pass visual map of the two committed CC0 images.
It helps a researcher open the image and find the surfaces described below.
It is a preprocessing observation, not a transcription or an OCR result.

本页登记两张已提交 CC0 图像的初步视觉区域，帮助研究者打开原图后定位
下述表面。它是预处理观察，不是摹写，也不是 OCR 结果。

The coordinate origin is the upper-left pixel of each native PNG. Bounding
boxes are approximate review boxes, not measured plate or line boundaries.
They were recorded from the full image at native dimensions on 2026-08-15.

坐标原点是各 PNG 原始尺寸的左上角。框选只是复核用近似区域，不是图版或
行界。它们依据 2026-08-15 对原始尺寸全图的直接查看记录。

## What this map does not establish / 本图不能证明什么

The regions do not establish line order, character boundaries, reading order,
or the identity of either displayed source string. No OCR, project
transcription, translation, component assignment, or decipherment was made.

这些区域不能证明行序、单字边界、阅读顺序或任一页面字符串的身份。本页没有
制作 OCR、项目摹写、翻译、构件分配或释读。

The image route is `public_domain_verified` for the two CC0 image files only.
Page text, catalog metadata, Heji references, and any future plate are separate
evidence and rights questions.

这里的 `public_domain_verified` 只适用于两张 CC0 图像文件。页面文字、著录
metadata、《合集》引文和未来图版仍是独立的证据与权利问题。
