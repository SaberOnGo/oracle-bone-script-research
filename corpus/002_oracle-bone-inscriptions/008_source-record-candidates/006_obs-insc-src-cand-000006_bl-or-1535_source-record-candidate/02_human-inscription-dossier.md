## Direct visual observations / 直接视觉观察

The locally checked image shows:

- an irregular, broad bone fragment on a light background;
- a narrower central strip with several dark round or oval pit marks;
- several incised marks visible along the central strip;
- a large plainer area toward the right side;
- broken and porous-looking edges at the fragment boundary.

These are image observations, not readings. The image orientation, side,
original object edge, and relation to any second fragment remain source-route
questions. No mark is assigned a modern character, pronunciation, meaning,
component, or inscription line.

This is not a project transcription.
