# Claim and evidence locator / 说法与证据定位

## Source-reported claims / 来源页面说法

- The database integrates form, sound, and meaning resources.
  Source: `About/About` and `guide/`.
- The database covers oracle bone, bronze, Warring States, small seal, and
  regular or clerical-script routes.
  Source: `About/About`, `guide/`, and `jiaguwen`.
- The current general statistics page reports 261,117 glyph forms,
  1,341,886 phonology records, and 366,530 indexes.
  Source: `Statistics/Statistics`, retrieved 2026-08-21.
- The oracle-bone page reports 2,548 character heads and 24,701 glyph forms.
  Source: `jiaguwen`, retrieved 2026-08-21.
- The license page states a CC0 dedication for queried glyph images and
  glyph-attribute information.
  Source: `License/License`.

## Independently checked observations / 独立核对观察

- The listed URLs returned HTML or PDF bytes on 2026-08-21.
- The local ignored snapshots have the sizes and SHA-256 values in
  `01_source-identity-and-access.md`.
- The oracle-bone page visibly exposes page, Heji, old-catalog, and group
  search concepts.
- The site also exposes a download page, a feedback route, and links to
  related form, sound, and dictionary resources.

## Not established by these pages / 这些页面不能证明

- that a returned glyph belongs to a specific excavated bone;
- that a catalog token is a confirmed Heji or plate identity;
- that a form-to-form link is an accepted historical evolution;
- that a modern character or pronunciation is the correct reading;
- that a database count is a stable release total;
- that third-party source images have the same rights as CC0 query images.
