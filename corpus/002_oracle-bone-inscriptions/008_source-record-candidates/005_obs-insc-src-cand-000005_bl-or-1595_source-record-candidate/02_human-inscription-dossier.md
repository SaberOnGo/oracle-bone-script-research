# Human inscription source dossier / 人类卜辞来源档案

## Source-reported object / 来源报告对象

The Commons pages describe a Chinese oracle bone supplied from the British
Library digital collections. They report a Shang-dynasty object dated to the
12th century BC and identify the collection as the British Library in London.
These are page-level descriptions, not an independently checked period or
excavation conclusion.

Commons 页面说明这是一件由大英图书馆数字馆藏提供的中国甲骨图像，
页面报告为商代、约公元前 12 世纪，馆藏为伦敦大英图书馆。这些只是
页面层面的来源描述，并非本项目独立核定的时期或出土结论。

## Source-displayed inscription strings / 来源页面显示文字

The recto page displays `已未庚申月㞢[食]` and gives an English gloss
about the moon being eclipsed between jiwei and gengshen. The verso page
displays `七日己未斲庚申月又食` and gives a corresponding source gloss.
Brackets and characters are preserved as displayed; they have not been
normalized by this project.

正面页面显示 `已未庚申月㞢[食]`，并给出关于己未与庚申之间月食的英文
说明。背面页面显示 `七日己未斲庚申月又食`，也给出对应的来源说明。
方括号和字形按页面原样保留；本项目没有规范化这些文字。

The Commons description says that modern scholars identify the event as the
eclipse of 27 December 1192 BC. That attribution is retained as
source-reported context only. No astronomical calculation, reading, or
dating claim is made here.

Commons 描述说现代学者把该事件认作公元前 1192 年 12 月 27 日的月食。
本档案只保留这一来源语境，不进行天文计算、释读或断代结论。

## Direct visual observations / 直接视觉观察

The recto image shows an irregular fragment with many incised signs and a
visible `1595` paper label. The verso shows the curved bone surface, drill
holes, porous texture, and additional incisions. These descriptions are
visual observations only. They do not segment characters or infer a reading.

正面图像可见不规则残片、多处刻痕以及明显的 `1595` 纸标签。背面可见
弧形骨面、钻凿痕、孔隙纹理和其他刻痕。这些只是视觉观察，不进行单字
分段，也不推定释读。
