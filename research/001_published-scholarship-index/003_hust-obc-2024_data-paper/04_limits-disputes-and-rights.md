# Limits, Disputes, And Rights / 限制、争议与权利

## Evidence Limits / 证据限制

- A cropped glyph image lacks the full bone, neighboring signs, crack layout,
  inscription text, catalog page, findspot, and collection history.
- A modern-character label is a dataset label, not a confirmed reading.
- A class merge is a preprocessing decision, not a variant judgment.
- Expert review is source-reported; item-level review records are unresolved.
- 单字裁图缺少完整甲骨、邻字、兆序、卜辞、著录页、出土和馆藏背景。
- 今字标签是数据标签，不是已确认释读。
- 类别合并是预处理决定，不是异体关系判断。
- 专家复核由论文报告；逐项复核记录仍待查。

## Recorded Disputes And Cautions / 已记录争议与风险

- The paper says undeciphered categories may contain duplicates.
- GuoXueDaShi-derived unreliable material is stored separately.
- Cross-source labels and merges require checks against the cited originals.
- No absence of a disagreement record should be treated as consensus.
- 论文称未释类别可能重复。
- 来自 GuoXueDaShi 的不可靠资料另存。
- 跨来源标签和合并须回到被引原始资料核查。
- 没有异议记录不等于已经形成共识。

本档案不把这些声明解释为第三方图书扫描、网页图像、数据库图像或所有
派生物的概括授权。

- Safe public derivative: bibliography, paraphrased method and limits,
  locator, citation relations, checksum routes, and review questions.
- Not copied here: article figures, tables, long text, or source glyph images.
- Third-party credit lines and original-source terms remain `unresolved`.
- 仓库状态：`source_marked_risk_noted`。
- 可安全公开：书目、自写方法和限制摘要、定位、引用关系、校验和路线及

- 第三方署名行和原始来源条款仍为 `unresolved`。
