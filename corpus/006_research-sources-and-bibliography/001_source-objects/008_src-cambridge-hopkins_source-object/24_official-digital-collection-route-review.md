# Official Digital Collection Route Review / 官方数字馆藏路线复核

## Purpose / 目的

This is a source-level human evidence note for opening the official digital
collection routes linked to the Cambridge/Hopkins finding list.

本卡片记录 Cambridge/Hopkins 清单所指向的官方数字馆藏入口，
供研究者逐一打开图像、物件记录和权利说明。它不是单字释读记录。

## Official Route Statements / 官方路线声明

- Finding-list page: Cambridge University Library, “Finding List for the
  Hopkins Collection of Chinese Oracle Bones”.
- The page states that Hopkins bones have been digitised and are available
  through the Chinese Works page; it also states that 50 bones have 3D
  formats.
- Chinese Works page: Cambridge Digital Library case-study route for the
  library's Chinese digital materials.
- Cambridge Digital Collections page: the 2025 WongAvery digital collection
  description lists 2D and 3D Hopkins oracle bones.

上述内容是官方页面的来源声明，不等于本项目已经打开了某一件物件、
图版、拓片或完整卜辞。数字化存在也不自动解决版权、物件对应或著录
互证问题。

## Route Records / 路线记录

- `src-cambridge-hopkins-finding-list`: finding-list and classified table.
- `src-cambridge-digital-chinese-works`: Chinese Works digital collection
  route named by the finding-list page.
- `src-cambridge-wongavery-digital-collection`: official 2025 description
  of the 2D/3D Hopkins digital collection route.
- `src-cambridge-hopkins-3d-cul52`: official CUL.52 3D exhibition route;
  this is a collection example, not an identity match for every row.

The source URLs are retained in the registered source and access records.
The current route labels are navigation evidence; they are not local image
assets and do not grant image reuse rights.

## Collection-Scale Statements / 馆藏规模声明

- One Cambridge University Library article reports 614 Chinese inscribed
  oracle bones in the collection and describes a CUL.52 3D model.
- The library's Chinese-collections introduction describes the Hopkins
  bequest as over 800 inscribed oracle bones.
- These are source-specific collection-scale statements from different
  institutional pages. They are preserved as an unresolved count relation,
  not averaged or converted into a project total.

## Human Review Requirements / 人工复核要求

- Open the official collection route and record the exact object identifier.
- Match CUL, Yingguo, Chalfant, and Heji references only after the object
  record, image direction, and catalog page have been compared.
- Record whether the route supplies a front image, reverse image, rubbing,
  3D model, plate number, page number, or only a collection description.
- Capture access date, rights statement, checksum, and local derivative path
  before saving any image or OCR output.
- Keep a collection-scale count difference separate from object identity,
  period assignment, transcription, reading, or decipherment.

## Boundary / 边界

This note is a source route and provenance record. It does not establish an
`obi-*` inscription identity, a character occurrence, a transcription, a
reading, a component, a variant, a date, or a decipherment conclusion.

本卡片只记录来源路线和追溯要求，不确认正式卜辞身份、字位、释文、
释读、构件、异体、断代或破译结论。
