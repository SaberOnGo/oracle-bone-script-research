# Limits, Disputes, And Rights / 限制、争议与权利

## Scholarly Limits / 学术限制

- Rubbings, facsimiles, and transcriptions have unequal evidentiary status.
- Pixel alignment does not prove a facsimile stroke exists on the rubbing.
- A group or order reflects an annotation decision, not final syntax.
- `Label`, `SubLabel`, and modern lookup labels are source records.
- `Mark` preserves an exception; it does not close the disagreement.
- 拓片、摹本和释文具有不同证据地位。
- 像素对齐不证明摹本笔画在拓片上存在。
- 分组和顺序反映标注决定，不是最终句法。
- `Label`、`SubLabel` 和今字检索标签只是来源记录。
- `Mark` 保存例外，不终结分歧。

All paper claims are author-reported and not a project conclusion.
所有论文说法均为作者报告，不是项目结论。

## Active Rights Conflict / 生效中的权利冲突

Three current statements are not interchangeable:

1. Hugging Face labels the dataset `CC BY 4.0`.
2. The official GitHub README says “academic research purposes only” and
   requires special permission for commercial use.
3. The article route is recorded locally as `CC BY-NC-ND 4.0`, with
   third-party material subject to separate credit-line terms.

三种表述不能互相替代：

1. Hugging Face 把数据集标为 `CC BY 4.0`。
2. 官方 GitHub README 称仅限学术研究，商业使用须获作者特别许可。
3. 本地权利复核记录文章为 `CC BY-NC-ND 4.0`，第三方材料另受署名行约束。

The effective repository status is `metadata_only_until_verified`. The older
`licensed_for_repository` value is historical and must not authorize reuse.

仓库生效状态为 `metadata_only_until_verified`。旧的
`licensed_for_repository` 只是历史记录，不能授权复用。

## Safe Public Form / 可安全公开形式

- Bibliography, paraphrased method, field guide, claim locator, checksum
  routes, conflict record, and concrete review questions.
- Do not reproduce paper figures, long text, rubbings, facsimiles, JSON rows,
  or sub-character images in this literature dossier.
- 可公开书目、自写方法、字段指南、说法定位、校验和路线、冲突记录和
  具体复核问题。
- 本档案不复制论文图、长文、拓片、摹本、JSON 行或构件图。

## Boundary / 边界

This review is not legal advice, rights clearance, a confirmed reading,
component assignment, inscription identity, or decipherment conclusion.

本复核不是法律意见、权利清理、已确认释读、构件归属、卜辞身份或破译
结论。
