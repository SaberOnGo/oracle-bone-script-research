## Direct visual observation / 直接视觉观察

The private high-definition view shows a pale plastron against a black
background. Broad cracks and missing areas divide the surface into many
irregular pieces. Dark linear marks, short repeated strokes, rectangular
forms, and black handwritten-looking annotations are visible. These are
pixel-level observations only. They do not identify signs, reading order,
publication plate, or inscription sentence.
