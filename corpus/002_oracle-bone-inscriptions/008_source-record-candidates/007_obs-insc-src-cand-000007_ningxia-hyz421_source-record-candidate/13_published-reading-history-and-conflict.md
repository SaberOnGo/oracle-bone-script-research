# Published reading history and conflict
# 公开释读史与冲突

## Result / 结果

A 2013 peer-reviewed article reports that `H3:421` contains an activity
described as avoiding rain at a place. The article attributes that statement
to Han Jiangsu's 2007 book, printed pages 351--353. This conflicts with the
current Schwartz route, which records two repeated entries about ritual food
from one ewe in Rong.

2013 年一篇同行评议论文报告：`H3:421` 含有在某地避雨的活动。该文把
这项说法追溯到韩江苏 2007 年专著第 351--353 页。这与当前 Schwartz 路线
形成冲突；后者记录两条重复辞例，内容涉及在戎用一只母羊制作祭祀食物。

This is a high-value conflict candidate. It is not a new project reading.
The 2003 source edition is now checked and supplies a direct baseline with
candidate counterevidence.
Han's pages remain unopened, so the project still must not decide whether Han
offered another interpretation or Shin made a numbering or citation error.

这是高价值冲突候选，不是本项目的新释读。2003 年底本现已核对，并形成
候选反证；韩江苏原书仍未打开，项目还不能判断韩是否另作解释，或申永子是否
误标编号、误引文献。

## Related `yi` dispute / 相关“宜”字争议

Zheng Banghong's 2012 note summarizes a wider disagreement. Yao Xuan treats
`yi` as offering viands, while Cao Dingyun and Han Jiangsu treat it as a
sacrifice name. Zheng also treats it as a sacrifice name but gives it a
benefactive construction in another inscription.

郑邦宏 2012 年文章整理了更广的分歧：姚萱把“宜”理解为进献肴肉，曹定云、
韩江苏把它视为祭名；郑邦宏也认作祭名，但在另一辞例中采用为动结构。

Review route / 复核路线:
<https://www.fdgwz.org.cn/Web/Show/1770>

That article is not a direct reading of HYZ 421. It only prevents the English
wording in Schwartz from being treated as an undisputed semantic conclusion.

该文不是对 HYZ 421 的直接释读。它只说明不能把 Schwartz 的英文措辞当作
没有争议的语义结论。
