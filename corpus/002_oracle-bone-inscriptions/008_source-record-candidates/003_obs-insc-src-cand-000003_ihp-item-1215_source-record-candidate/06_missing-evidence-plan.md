# Missing evidence plan / 缺失证据计划

## Blocking checks / 阻断检查

1. Refresh the checksum-bound page snapshot and compare its `.fr-view`
   anchors, access date, byte count, and SHA-256.
2. Confirm the three image routes and their rights terms with the museum.
3. Locate the `Yi Bian` references in an independent catalog and record
   volume, page, plate, and exact locator.
4. Determine whether the displayed short text is complete, partial, or a
   catalog annotation.
5. Obtain an authorized rubbing or plate view with stable image provenance.
6. Build OCR or a manual transcription only after the plate is located.
7. Record alternative readings, proposers, and contrary readings separately.
8. Link characters only after sign-level visual comparison.

## 具体待查问题

1. 更新带校验和的页面快照，并比较 `.fr-view` 定位、访问日期、字节数和 SHA-256。
2. 向馆方核对三条图像路线及其权利条件。
3. 在独立著录中定位 `Yi Bian` 引文，记录卷、页、图版和精确位置。
4. 查明页面短文字是完整文字、残缺文字还是著录注释。
5. 取得有授权且来源明确的拓片或图版。
6. 图版定位后再建立 OCR 或人工摹写。
7. 分别记录不同释读、提出者和反对意见。
8. 逐字视觉比较后才建立单字关联。

## Release boundary / 发布边界

Until the checks close, status remains
`source_record_candidate_needs_catalog_and_text_review`; no image, OCR, or
character claim may be promoted.

在检查闭合前，状态保持
`source_record_candidate_needs_catalog_and_text_review`，不得提升图像、OCR
或单字结论。
