## Falsification And Reopen Conditions / 证伪与重开条件

- Withdraw the object-to-plate route if an independent edition gives a
  different item, accession, period, or plate mapping.
- Withdraw any front/reverse linkage if a catalog proves the two JPEG routes
  depict different objects or later repairs rather than two views.
- Withdraw any text alignment if a cited plate or rubbing places the lines or
  placeholders differently from the museum page.
- Reopen C5--C7 only after a cited plate locator, line-addressable text,
  exact glyph positions, and a dated disagreement or reading-history route
  are recorded.

- 如果独立版本把对象、登记号、时期或图版对应到其他记录，撤回对象—图版路线。
- 如果著录证明两条 JPEG 不是同一对象的正反面，撤回正反面关联。
- 如果有出处的图版或拓片改变行位或占位，撤回任何文字对齐。
- 只有记录带出处的图版定位、可定位文字、精确字位和有日期的争议或释读史
  后，才重开 C5—C7。

本页不确认馆藏身份、卜辞身份、释文、释读、构件归属或破译结论。
