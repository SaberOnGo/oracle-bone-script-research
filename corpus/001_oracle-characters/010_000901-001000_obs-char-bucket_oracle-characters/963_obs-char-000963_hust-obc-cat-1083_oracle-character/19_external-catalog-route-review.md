## What The Network Evidence Shows / 网络证据说明

headwords or text contexts. A catalog number identifies a bone, fragment,
inscription, or edition route; it does not identify every sign shown on that
record. The HUST filename therefore remains a locator candidate only.

文件名仍只能作为定位候选。
