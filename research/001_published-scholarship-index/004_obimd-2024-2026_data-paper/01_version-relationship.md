# Version Relationship / 版本关系

## What Can Be Linked / 可以确认的关联

Both records describe the named OBIMD dataset, use the same arXiv project
lineage, and route readers to the KLOBIP/OBIMD data and libang1991/OBIMD code.
The 2026 article is the current peer-reviewed publication cited by the official
GitHub README.

两份记录均描述 OBIMD 数据集，属于同一 arXiv 项目脉络，并指向
KLOBIP/OBIMD 数据和 libang1991/OBIMD 代码。官方 GitHub README 当前引用
2026 年同行评议论文。

## Material Differences / 实质差异

- Title changed from *Oracle Bone Inscriptions Multi-modal Dataset* to a title
  emphasizing contextual interpretation.
- The arXiv record lists 20 authors; the journal record lists 13.
- The journal record names Bang Li and Jing Yang as equal contributors and
  Donghao Luo and Taisong Jin as corresponding authors.
- The journal article expands acquisition, role-based verification, field
  definitions, technical benchmarks, and modern-label cautions.
- The journal article clarifies 9,913 Heji rubbings plus 164 Huayuanzhuang East
  rubbings within the reported total of 10,077.
- 题名从一般“多模态数据集”改为强调语境解释。
- arXiv 列 20 位作者，期刊版本列 13 位。
- 期刊版注明 Bang Li、Jing Yang 同等贡献，通讯作者为 Donghao Luo、
  Taisong Jin。
- 期刊版扩充采集、分级复核、字段定义、技术基准和今字标签警示。
- 期刊版把 10,077 总数说明为 9,913 片《合集》资料加 164 片花东资料。

They are not byte-identical versions and must not share page or section
locators. 它们不是字节相同版本，不能共用页码或章节定位。

## Citation Decision / 引用决定

Use the 2026 article for peer-reviewed claims and current field definitions.
Use arXiv:2407.03900 when documenting the earlier public state, its author
list, or changes in scope. Cite both when the version history itself matters.

同行评议说法和当前字段定义引用 2026 论文；记录早期公开状态、作者名单或
范围变化时引用 arXiv:2407.03900；研究版本史时并列引用两者。

This relation is independently checked at the project-record level. It does
not prove that every dataset file remained unchanged between releases.

项目记录层面的关系已独立核查，但不能证明各次发布间每个数据文件都未变。
