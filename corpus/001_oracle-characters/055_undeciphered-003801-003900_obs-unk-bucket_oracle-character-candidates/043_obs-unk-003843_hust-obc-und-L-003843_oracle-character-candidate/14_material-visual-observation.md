English:
Several narrow traces converge near the center. One runs diagonally toward the
left, another rises above the center, and a longer stroke angles down toward
the right. A further trace descends below the crossing. The strokes are dark
against a light, mostly even background.

中文：
几条较窄痕迹在中心附近汇合。一条向左斜出，另一条从中心向上延伸，还有一
条较长痕迹向右下方倾斜。交汇处下方另有痕迹继续向下。痕迹在较均匀的明亮
背景上呈深色。
