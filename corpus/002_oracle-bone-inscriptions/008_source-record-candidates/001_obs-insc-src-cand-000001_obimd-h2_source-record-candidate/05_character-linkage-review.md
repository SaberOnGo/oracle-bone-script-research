## Falsification Checks / 反证检查

- Does the UID crop disagree between rubbing and facsimile?
- Does the source UID image family contain materially different shapes?
- Does an independent transcription omit, dispute, or reorder the position?
- Is a box damaged, joined, or split differently in another edition?
- 拓片与摹本中的 UID 截图是否不一致？
- 来源 UID 图像族是否包含实质不同的字形？
- 独立释文是否省略、争议或重排该位置？
- 另一版本是否对字框作了不同的残损、合并或切分？
