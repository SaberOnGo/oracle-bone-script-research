## Blocked transfer / 暂停转移

Do not transfer the book's translation, a modern reading, a character link,
an inscription identity, a period, or a component claim into project records
until the permitted edition, plate, source object, and disagreement trail are
opened and compared.

在打开并比较获准版本、图版、来源实物和不同意见路线前，不得把书中译文、今字
释读、单字关联、卜辞身份、年代或构件主张转入项目记录。

The route can support a future human review; it cannot by itself create an
`obi-*` record or an `ai_adjudicated_candidate`.

该路线可以支持未来人类复核，但不能单独创建 `obi-*` 记录或
`ai_adjudicated_candidate`。
