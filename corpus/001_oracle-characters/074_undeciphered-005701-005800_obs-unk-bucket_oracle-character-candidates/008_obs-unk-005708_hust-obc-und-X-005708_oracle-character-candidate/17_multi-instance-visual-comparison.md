## Cross-group Comparison / 跨组比较

The most frequent visual scaffold is a tall central descent, paired upper side
strokes, and a basal fork, enclosure, or stacked chevrons. It is clearest in
groups `1180`, `1181`, `1182`, `1184`, and `1185`.

最常见的视觉骨架是高长中央下行笔、成对上部侧笔，以及下部分叉、围合或层叠
斜枝；这一模式在 `1180`、`1181`、`1182`、`1184`、`1185` 组最清楚。

That recurrence is only evidence for auditing the HUST grouping. A central
vertical is a low-specificity feature. Groups `1179`, `1186`, `1187`, and
`1188` contain members whose cross-arms, broad lobes, or irregular side fields
are not explained by the shared central line alone.

这种重复只支持审计 HUST 分组。中央纵笔的排他性很低；`1179`、`1186`、
`1187`、`1188` 组中存在横贯笔、宽叶状区或不规则侧部笔群，仅凭中央纵笔
不能解释这些差异。
