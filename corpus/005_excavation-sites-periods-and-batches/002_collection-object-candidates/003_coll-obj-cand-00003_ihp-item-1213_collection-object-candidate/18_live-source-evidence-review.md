## Text quality and provenance / 文本质量与来源

The page supplies a short front/reverse display and an English description,
but the diviner name and several signs are image placeholders. It does not
provide a page-level publication locator, complete OCR, or an independent
edition citation in the current route. The text status is therefore
`source_reported_partial_transcription_with_placeholders`.

页面提供正面、背面短文字和英文说明，但贞人名称及若干字为图像占位。当前路由没有
提供出版物页码、完整 OCR 或独立版本引文。因此文本状态为
`source_reported_partial_transcription_with_placeholders`。

The source text, English description, image URLs, local JPEG checksums, and
future HTML capture must remain separate evidence records. Their co-occurrence
does not prove a complete plate transcription or a settled reading history.

来源文字、英文说明、图像 URL、本地 JPEG 校验和及未来 HTML 抓取必须保持为分开的
证据记录。它们同时出现不等于完整图版释文，也不等于释读史已经确定。
